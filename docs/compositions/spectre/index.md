# :material-ghost-outline: Spectre

A compelling apparition still needs a door through which its visitors can leave. Spectre is the Virtual Reality Composition: it admits a virtual place as `VRHabitat@1`, then keeps one bounded **Encounter**—participants, purpose, consent, significant events, interruption, and exit—inside it.

The name follows _spectre_, an appearance or apparition. The experience can matter without becoming Persona identity, physical truth, or authority. An Avatar may present the Lich there; Avatar retains presentation and binding membership while Spectre keeps the place and meeting.

## Contract

`spectre.virtual_reality` revision `2` publishes `spectre.admit_habitat@1` and `spectre.enter_encounter@2`. It returns immutable `VRHabitat@1` or settled `SpectreEncounter@2`: completed, safely exited, interrupted, refused, or unresolved. World authorship, persistent-world effects, headset/runtime ownership, raw tracking archives, Persona/Avatar ownership, generic multiplayer, public release, and claims of physical presence remain outside its contract.
