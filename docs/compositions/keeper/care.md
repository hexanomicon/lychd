# Keeper care

A missed due window is unfinished care, even when its reminder has expired.
`keeper.record_care@1` records `KeeperCareEvent@1` and `KeeperCareResult@1`, with an optional
`KeeperYieldReceipt@1` for confirmed collected material.

## Record what happened

For feeding completion, water inspection, cleaning, confirmed transfer, appointments, or other
care, record what was intended alongside what happened. Preserve the subject and placement
references, due window, actor, effective and recorded times, evidence, uncertainty, and outcome.
If professional instructions guided the work, link their exact reference and version. Recording
them does not broaden their authority: clinical diagnosis and treatment decisions remain with
the external professional.

Physical work belongs to a human or separately admitted owner. Local controller safety wins.
Distinguish proposed, confirmed, partial, missed, cancelled, interrupted, and unknown-effect work.
A lost acknowledgment cannot justify repeating feeding, cleaning, or a transfer. Reconcile the
effect and exact event identities first; record unresolved effects explicitly.

## Receive collected material

A yield receipt records confirmed eggs, milk, or other collected material with source individual
or group, collection-event identity and revision, actor, effective and recorded times, material,
quantity and units, condition and uncertainty, and disposition. A hen
group can remain a durable subject while successive collections produce separate receipts.
Neither birth nor death automatically creates a product or stock lot.

[Homestead](../homestead/index.md) separately accepts exact sourced lots, referencing the receipt.
Keeper's collection record supplies no food-safety or consumption verdict and does not equate
collection with acceptance into stores.
Duplicate receipt delivery cannot create another collection or stock lot. Amendments preserve
the original collection identity and trigger an explicit stock adjustment against prior acceptance.

## Finish or hand off

Close the invocation with the confirmed result, named partial completion, refusal, blockage, or
unresolved physical effect. A care schedule can identify future work after this invocation ends.
On restart, recover its settled result and reconcile pending effects before retrying.

Deletion also requires attention to unfinished care. Stop schedules and disclosure, then reconcile
or hand off pending obligations before authorized erasure. The animal's custody and welfare
obligations remain even when records are removed.
