# :material-folder-account-outline: Cases

A `ServiceCase` records one client purpose and its requested action: check existing PZP, explain a
product, compare eligible offers, prepare a renewal, correct a fact, hand work to a human, or report
why the office cannot proceed. The action belongs to the case; it is not a separate application.

## Contact to outcome

1. Preserve the native contact identity, time, attachments, provenance, and deduplication evidence.
2. Resolve the sender only to the assurance the channel proves. Familiar email, caller ID, or voice
   does not authorize a protected read.
3. Open or match a case and pin purpose, requested action, business policy, product releases, staff
   assignment, deadlines, consent, and current authority.
4. Validate identifiers, dates, money, required fields, eligibility, payload closure, and effect
   identity with deterministic tools. A Mind may extract, explain, and draft; it cannot invent a
   premium, exclusion, client need, provider decision, or completed act.
5. Review the exact answer or prepared effect. Changed payload means a new revision and review.
6. Submit an admitted effect once. Unknown acknowledgement parks the same case for reconciliation.

Cases retain facts by provenance: imported, client-stated, staff-confirmed, model-proposed,
deterministically derived, or provider-observed. A summary never replaces source evidence.

Each case names current owner, wait, deadline, pinned revisions, and terminal or partial outcome.
Silence is unknown. After restart, stale authority, expired quote, unavailable Pattern, or uncertain
provider effect parks for review rather than replaying conversation.

Consent to converse, store records, record a call, disclose fields, and perform a financial effect
are separate. Revision one admits no medical record, credit judgment, affordability decision,
payment, signature, or policy binding.

Continue with [Products](products.md) for the knowledge used by the case or [Channels](channels.md)
for its contact and delivery boundary.
