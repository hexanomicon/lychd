# :material-source-merge: Synthesizer

An old conversation asks for brief answers; a later project calls for detailed explanations.
Synthesizer would relate those statements to their dates and purposes, preserving both until
evidence or review supports a scoped preference. It is the interpretation and synthesis stage of
the [Transmuter candidate](index.md), working from [Extractor](extractor.md) outputs.

## Compare and propose

Each synthesis attempt pins the selected extracted units, explicitly authorized comparison
material, purpose, target namespaces, synthesis implementation and configuration revisions, and
resource limits. Missing authorization excludes the comparison source or destination. Similarity
does not grant access to another person's or Persona's records.

The output groups evidence into tentative claims, relations, contradictions, and proposed uses.
Each proposal names its subject and receiving owner, exact proposed content or change, supporting
and opposing source references, uncertainty, dependencies, and required review. A Persona-change
proposal names its exact parent revision. A summary retains its derivation rather than becoming
a substitute source.

The stage keeps three subjects distinct: the operator's preferences and history, the Lich's
Persona and commitments, and dated facts about devices or software. A model-generated description
of the operator remains that model's interpretation. Repetition cannot turn it into an endorsed
preference. Material may remain a source-linked knowledge candidate without any request to change
memory or identity.

## Receiving owners

| Proposed use | Receiving decision |
| --- | --- |
| Retain or correct a memory | [Archive](../../adr/27-memory.md) owns Memory admission, curation, and recall eligibility. |
| Revise the Lich's Persona | [Mirror](../../adr/32-identity.md) owns the reviewed revision and its publication. |
| Nominate material for training | [Soulforge](../../adr/33-training.md) independently admits a corpus and any later training. |
| Request a missing parser, tool, or code candidate | [Smith](../../adr/35-assimilation.md) supplies candidate authorship through a separately admitted exact application contract. |

Settled Smith outputs may also enter Transmuter as attributed material for interpretation. Their
authorship establishes no promotion or truth. Forge constructs packages and images; Soulforge
owns training. Neither code activation nor weight promotion follows automatically from synthesis.
This study specifies these proposed handoffs without publishing executable contracts for them.

## Account for the result

The [dossier](index.md#the-processing-dossier) links each fixed proposal to its handoff and the
receiving owner's outcome. Local synthesis may finish while memory is accepted, a Persona change
is refused, and training was never requested. An unknown downstream effect remains unknown until
reconciled with that owner; a missing receipt is not permission to repeat the effect.

Retries preserve fixed inputs and revision pins. Source drift or correction creates revised
candidates and invalidates affected pending proposals. Past acceptances remain recorded; needed
repairs are proposed to the responsible owner rather than silently rewriting its history.

Budget exhaustion retains completed proposals and identifies unfinished work. Cancellation stops
new synthesis and handoffs while preserving committed effects. Resumption must reconcile recorded
uncertainty and recheck authority before further work. The exact executable recovery contract
remains acceptance work under [Workflow](../../adr/28-workflow.md).
