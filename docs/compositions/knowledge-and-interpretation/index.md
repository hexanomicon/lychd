# Knowledge & Interpretation

Work through imported material to establish what its sources say, who produced each claim, and
which conclusions their evidence supports. Preserve conflicting accounts, uncertain interpretations,
and the route back to correction. The work can begin with a personal archive, a research collection,
or a recorded working session.

| Composition | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| [Transmuter](transmuter/index.md) | An attributed processing dossier, source-grounded knowledge, and proposals for separately admitted use. |

A completed dossier may preserve a useful interpretation without proposing a change to memory or
identity. [Transmuter's receiving owners](transmuter/synthesizer.md#receiving-owners) independently
decide whether to adopt each proposal. [State of Work](../../state-of-the-work.md#composition-portfolio-delivery)
records implementation and delivery.
