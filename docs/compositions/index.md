# :material-shape-outline: Compositions

A publication needs sources, editorial judgment, and a release decision. A musical work needs its own acceptance, credits, and production records. Combining them should preserve what each contribution means and who answers for it.

A **Composition** gives each application a durable home. For the publication, Broadcast keeps
the source words and evidence alongside editorial decisions and publication candidates. Riffmaw
keeps the musical work's production history and acceptance. Atelier's editor places the accepted
music against the picture, judges the final mix, and finishes the local video master. Broadcast
can prepare that master for a named audience. Release requires a separate decision.

More generally, a Composition keeps the application's records, judges its results, and governs
the effects it may request. Its policies, projections, and catalogue of Patterns belong to the
same reusable application across customers, Products, and deployments. Browse by the outcome
you want to leave behind.

For a software question, [Creation investigation](creation/index.md) retains the source, candidate
and separate verdict under one bounded application contract.

## The Portfolio

These **Native Reference Compositions** are accepted first-party application contracts and worked examples. [State of Work](../state-of-the-work.md#composition-portfolio-delivery) collects their implementation evidence.

### Areas

An **Area** groups Compositions by the kind of work you want to find. Each Area has an index
leading to its applications and their outcomes.

| Area | Enter through |
| --- | --- |

| [Stewardship & Acquisition](stewardship-and-acquisition/index.md) | personal health and wellbeing, places, animal care, cultivation, and acquisition |
| [Presence & Embodiment](presence-and-embodiment/index.md) | social presence, presentation, virtual worlds, physical bodies, and mobile sessions |
| [Professional Operations](professional-operations/index.md) | client casework, business books, technical service, and public-source software investigations |
| [Knowledge & Interpretation](knowledge-and-interpretation/index.md) | attributed source interpretation, synthesis, and correction |
| [Security & Trust](security-and-trust/index.md) | security casework and compute-node privacy qualification |

Areas may overlap because a reader can approach the same work from different concerns. Each
Composition nevertheless keeps one canonical route and owns its records and judgment; the Area
only helps the reader find it. A [Suite](products-and-suites.md#compositions-relate-without-nesting)
coordinates live work within one Area or across several. The [Area contract](../adr/28-workflow.md#areas-for-discovery)
keeps that navigation separate from application ownership. Guides explain how to design, implement,
and combine applications.

## Follow a shared work

[Canvas](canvas/index.md) keeps visual boards, original ink and reviewed transformations.
[Atelier](atelier/index.md) produces accepted visuals and can finish a local video. An editor
places settled [Riffmaw music](riffmaw/index.md) and [Language Edition dialogue and captions](language-edition/index.md)
on Atelier's timeline, adds picture sound, and renders an accepted `VideoMaster@1`. The work
can end there. [Broadcast](broadcast/index.md) packages the exact master for an audience and
outlet. Its [release](broadcast/release.md) is a separate external act.

The direction can change without erasing earlier work. A locked Atelier master and a commissioned
Broadcast script can enter Language Edition. If the new performance needs a longer shot, Atelier
accepts the timing change in a successor master. Each owner preserves its records and approvals.

Settled artifacts connect these applications directly. A result that must coordinate several
owners' live Invocations—commissioning work, awaiting revisions, or recovering their execution—
needs a **Suite**.
The [worked handoffs](products-and-suites.md#compositions-relate-without-nesting) extend this
relationship to household care, embodied presence, and professional casework. A **Product** gives
the resulting application or Suite a named operator promise.

## The Composition test

For a new idea, ask what should remain when the conversation ends and who can judge it finished.
Then try the difficult endings: work refused, partly completed, stopped, or interrupted with an
effect still unknown. Who keeps that account and decides how to recover? [Choosing a Home](choosing-a-home.md)
tests whether those responsibilities need a Composition or already belong to a Product, profile,
Pattern, projection, mechanism, reference, or Suite.
