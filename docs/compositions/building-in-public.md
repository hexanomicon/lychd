# :material-hammer-wrench: Building in Public: The Tutorial Forge

**Candidate question:** can one evidence-grounded vertical slice become a truthful tutorial
without manufacturing progress?

| Local maturity | Candidate season | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unaccepted candidate study** | one vertical slice | six movements, each an accountable episode |

The Tutorial Forge treats a tutorial as a receipt. It follows one real form from uncertainty to
bounded evidence, including the first design that failed and the human decisions that changed it.
It is not a polished reconstruction that claims the struggle never happened.

## A season has six movements

1. State the human problem, non-goals, and smallest proving scenario.
2. Name the owner and exact boundary under examination.
3. Show the first design and the evidence that forced a change.
4. Implement one vertical slice at an exact repository revision.
5. Test failure, interruption, recovery, authority, and effects in proportion to that slice.
6. Publish receipts, limits, corrections, and the observed result.

Prototyping may wander. The account must distinguish observation, inference, design, and myth;
each episode pins the repository revision and execution boundary, commands, focused tests,
receipts, provenance, instructive failure, material model assistance, human decisions, and later
corrections.

## Candidate subjects

| Season | Minimum truthful slice |
| --- | --- |
| [Voidlight Studio](voidlight-studio.md) | Frozen references and style bible through a `CreativeAssetBundle@1`: concept, sprite/texture, audio, provenance, deterministic normalization, review/repair. |
| [Blockworld Inhabitant](blockworld-inhabitant.md) | Private server and bounded bot: container/Rune, observation/action, snapshots, `observe → plan → act → verify`, consent, restart, and proof that no block changed outside its plot. |
| [Health, Food & Movement](health-food-and-movement.md) | Synthetic local profile, hard restrictions, units and three-state allergen closure, solver, approval/reflection, restart, export/deletion, network disabled. |
| [Game Foundry](game-foundry.md) | “Make me a game with rockets” from `voidlight.game-suite` charcoal through typed assets, engine import, a bounded change, build/playtest/repair, and a local release candidate. |
| [Broadcast Studio](broadcast-studio.md) | Source dossier and claim ledger through a Voidlight request, editorial/narration/timeline/render/accessibility/rights review, ending at a local publication candidate. |

It must not invent unexecuted commands, turn a diagram into a feature announcement, erase failed
tests or manual edits, hide material model or asset assistance, or introduce public exposure,
sensitive recommendations, credentials, or irreversible effects for drama. A truthful slice does
not prove that every future host will behave alike.
