# :material-compass-outline: Campaign

A campaign states what is being acquired and what would count as an honest finish before any
source opens. `scavenger.plan_campaign@1` pins desired outcome, existing inventory, hard criteria,
weighted preferences, budget, deadline, region, evidence tier, delivery or travel semantics,
disclosure policy, and one operating mode.

## Subject profiles

| Profile | Distinct judgment | Revision-one consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bazaar** | identity, condition, attributable seller claims, landed cost, and bounded bargaining for Bazoš-style listings | terms, one approved COD commitment, parcel inspection, dispute, or refusal |
| **Technology** | exact CPU, board, BIOS, cooling, memory, GPU, storage, case, PSU, firmware, and connector compatibility | Bazaar consequence only after compatibility and evidence are freshly revalidated |
| **Property** | transaction type, area, rooms, locality, route and amenity observations, listing contradictions, and professional questions | shortlist and diligence packet only; no contact, viewing, offer, reservation, deposit, signature, or payment |

A subject profile is a versioned rule set inside Scavenger, not another Composition or a source
adapter. A technology profile may refine a Bazaar subject; property rules never leak into hardware
or parcel judgment. Selecting a profile grants no source, credential, private anchor, money, or
effect authority.

`Watch` observes and reports. `Concierge` may prepare questions and offers for review. `Bounded
Autopilot` may act only inside a fully pinned envelope. Changing the subject, ceiling, permitted
disclosure, payment rail, evidence requirement, or deadline creates a new campaign revision rather
than silently widening the old one.

Continue with [Market](market.md) once the campaign is closed enough to measure candidates.
