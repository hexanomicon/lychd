# :material-package-down: Acquisition

A parcel arrives with the right tracking number and amount. Its contents and condition still need inspection. `scavenger.receive@1` compares carrier, tracking, amount, expected identity, and package notes, then records acceptance, rejection, dispute, return, or loss. Cash on delivery (COD) alone proves neither what is inside nor its condition; the Magus makes the final payment, acceptance, dispute, and return decisions.

To judge the parcel, return to the exact terms freshly authorized through `scavenger.commit@1`. Property revision one never reaches that effect: it ends at a diligence packet, without contact, viewing, offer, reservation, deposit, signature, or settlement.

Retain enough attribution to follow the parcel back to the decision: campaigns, snapshots, score inputs, seller claims, messages, approvals, commitments, parcels, inspections, disputes, and diligence questions stay separately identifiable. Checkpoints pin Pattern, source, selector, catalogue, parser, template, and policy revisions so recovery can reconstruct the work it is resuming.

On restart, reuse the pinned observations and idempotency keys. Park the occurrence if source state has changed, and reconcile an unknown commitment or send before retrying. For export, keep private anchors, messages, evidence, and delivery data separate. For deletion, disable schedules/sessions and inventory open commitments and disputes; removing local material cannot erase the seller's copy.
