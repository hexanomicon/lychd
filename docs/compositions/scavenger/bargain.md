# :material-handshake-outline: Bargain

`scavenger.negotiate@1` asks approved questions or makes offers inside one immutable bargaining
envelope. The envelope pins candidate, missing evidence, opening and all-in ceilings, currency,
delivery constraints, concessions, truthful automation disclosure, prohibited claims, caps,
expiry, and stop signals.

Refusal, opt-out, abuse, silence, sale, changed subject, changed payment rail, failed evidence,
expiry, or exhausted caps closes automatic messaging. A seller cannot talk Scavenger into a wider
budget, a different item, more private data, or an unapproved payment method.

Every send carries a stable effect identity and redacted receipt. A missing acknowledgement becomes
`unknown_send`; restart parks the thread and neither resends nor creates a second commitment until
the destination is reconciled. Seller replies remain attributable hostile input, never new policy.

The outcome is exact terms, unanswered questions, refusal, expiry, or an offer eligible for fresh
human review. Bargaining itself never authorizes commitment or payment.

Continue with [Acquisition](acquisition.md) only after current evidence, terms, budget, exposure,
authority, delivery method, and disclosure are revalidated.
