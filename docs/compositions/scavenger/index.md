# :material-magnify: Scavenger

The cheapest listing may be the wrong part; the attractive vehicle may carry unanswered questions. Scavenger keeps a finite acquisition campaign open to inspection until its evidence supports a decision, a commitment, a parcel outcome, or an honest stopping point.

## Contract

`scavenger` revision `3` publishes `scavenger.plan_campaign@1`, `scavenger.observe_market@2`, `scavenger.qualify_candidate@1`, `scavenger.rank_candidates@1`, `scavenger.negotiate@1`, `scavenger.commit@1`, and `scavenger.receive@1`.

A campaign begins with its revisioned subject profile, concrete need, known inventory, budget, deadline, region, evidence standard, source policy, and autonomy ceiling. It may return a durable campaign, reviewed field, explicit preference revision, shortlist, seller thread, negotiated outcome, commitment, parcel result, or diligence packet.

Scavenger retains the campaign and the criteria, observations, evidence, rankings, judgments, preference revisions, offers, commitments, parcels, inspections, and diligence that support its decisions. Scout separately owns the bounded acquisition/interaction receipts. The Magus decides private disclosure, final commitment, payment, acceptance, dispute, and every property transaction; a campaign result cannot make those decisions on its own.

Unbounded scraping, spam, false identity or leverage, hidden criteria changes, professional certification, and unapproved commitment, payment, deposit, signature, or settlement remain outside the contract. Scavenger searches a finite market; [Hunter](../../sepulcher/extensions/shadow/hunter.md) instead adversarially challenges an already formed candidate.

## Revision continuity

Revision 2 selected `scavenger.observe_market@1` alongside the other six Pattern references.
It remains the historical finite-market design; its meaning is not rewritten into a new custody
or recovery protocol. Revision 3 keeps the application and those other references, and selects
`scavenger.observe_market@2` for the explicit [finite feed observation](market.md#finite-feed-observation)
request, retained whole field, deletion and recovery contract. The direct complete-seed admission
is part of that successor, not evidence that `plan_campaign` executed.

The tracked registry and application stores have no revision-2 Scavenger implementation or
executable data migration. This records that source boundary; it does not claim knowledge of
private implementations. [State](../../state-of-the-work.md#composition-portfolio-delivery)
separately records registration and native acceptance. The first observation profile does not
implement qualification, ranking, bargaining, commitment, receipt or preference learning.

## Open the campaign

[Campaign](campaign.md) fixes the need and permitted consequence. [Market](market.md) follows
the whole candidate field into evidence, ranking, and correction; [Bargain](bargain.md) takes approved questions to a seller.
When exact terms are ready, [Acquisition](acquisition.md) follows commitment into receipt,
inspection, dispute, or diligence.
