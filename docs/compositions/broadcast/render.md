# :material-movie-cog-outline: Render

Atelier pins the renderer, fonts, codecs, filters, source assets and timeline, then probes the
exact export and records its final review. Follow [Render and review](../atelier/master.md#render-and-review)
for the production contract and recovery after a missing write acknowledgement.

Broadcast revision `4` rendered its editorial package. Revision `5` checks the accepted
master against a channel's audience and delivery requirements. Unsupported aspect, duration or
audio layout goes back to Atelier for a new master. A package ready for one channel may still
need changes for another. [Distribution](distribution.md)
