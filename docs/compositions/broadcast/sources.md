# :material-file-document-multiple-outline: Sources

Sources establishes what Broadcast may claim before polish makes weak evidence persuasive. It owns
the editorial evidence set and claim lineage; it does not certify the entire world behind a source.

## Freeze the evidence

Only named snapshots enter the project. Each records the source identity, captured revision or
digest, relevant span, author or publisher when known, classification, rights posture, intended
audience, permitted purpose, disclosures, and retention. A live URL can support capture, but it is
not the frozen evidence.

The editorial brief pins the question, audience, target, scope, tone, exclusions, review standard,
and freshness boundary. A materially changed question or source set creates a forward revision.

## Build the claim ledger

Every factual claim binds to one or more source spans and records whether it is quotation,
extraction, calculation, synthesis, or interpretation. The record keeps confidence, assumptions,
contradicting evidence, reviewer state, and each article, script, caption, or timeline object that
depends on it.

A weak, stale, disputed, or ungrounded claim remains marked. It may be narrowed, attributed,
removed, returned for research, or refused; it is never upgraded by fluent wording. A later
correction appends a corrected or withdrawn state and marks dependants stale without rewriting the
historical claim.

Source rights, privacy, likeness, and Portal egress are evaluated where the material enters and
again where the intended use changes. The resulting evidence and claim revisions pass to
[Script](script.md). They carry no permission to synthesize a voice, alter another Composition's
asset, render, or publish.
