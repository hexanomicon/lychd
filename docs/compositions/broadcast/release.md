# :material-publish: Release

The package can remain local and still be finished. Release begins when an approving Principal
authorizes one exact payload for a particular audience and destination. Sending a commissioned
video privately to its named requester follows the separately authorized [Intercom
return](../atelier/master.md#return-the-master).

## Review before effect

Review source support, claims, voice and likeness authority, privacy and egress limits,
accessibility and the selected content. Bind the channel package, master or article digest,
destination account, audience, visibility, disclosures, schedule, cost and approving Principal.
An upload to a private draft area and publication to a public feed need independent authority.

For each effect pin the payload and request digests, idempotency key, destination identity,
adapter and reconciliation lookup. Where a [Reach channel](../reach/social.md) implements the
authorized transport, pass only its exact delivery request and permitted bytes. Retain its
provider observation with Broadcast's publication record and decision. A standalone Reach
post can have its own separate authority. The current Reach social builders prepare organic
text. Publishing a video through such a channel still needs a qualified media adapter and
destination receipt.

Suppose the destination accepts an upload and the connection drops before the receipt returns.
Record the outcome as **unknown**. Resolve the pinned lookup to discover whether those bytes
arrived. A browser upload could create a second copy. A retry still needs the authorized effect.

A correction or removal names the publication and exact destination receipt it changes. Preserve
the original, record the forward correction or takedown request, authorize its effect and
reconcile the result. A factual change returns to the source and script owner. A changed video
requires a successor Atelier master and renewed channel review. Refusal, expired authority,
a changed candidate or ambiguous remote state each leaves its own disposition.

Recovery resolves the pinned Pattern, package, sources, script, accepted master and target
adapter before consulting destination receipts. Keep producer evidence reachable under its
own retention policy. Deletion inventories derivatives, requests authorized downstream removal,
and preserves a content-free receipt. Feeds and caches may still retain copies.

## Proving package

Use a sourced article and an accepted short master to prepare two channel packages. Check the
article's claims against their spans. Have one channel accept the exact master and a second
request a portrait cut. Admit the returned successor only for its declared channel. Exercise
expired release approval, a lost upload acknowledgement and a later correction with a test
destination. Record which package remained local, which destination confirmed receipt, and
what remained uncertain. [Portfolio delivery record](../../state-of-the-work.md#composition-portfolio-delivery)
