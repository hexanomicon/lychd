# :material-script-text-play-outline: Script

The canonical sentence must remain readable before it is spoken over an image. Script owns the article, narration, dialogue, title, description, and formatted variants from which spoken delivery and captions derive.

Choose exact claim revisions from the [ledger](sources.md), preserving attribution, uncertainty, and disclosure. A sentence joining several claims keeps every dependency. A shorter channel version may alter structure or length while retaining source meaning, audience, and claim scope.

Approve narration and caption words before assembly. `LanguageVersionRequest@2` can send [Language Edition](../language-edition/index.md) the exact `ScriptRevision@1`, locked media digest, target language/timebase, pronunciation/performance direction, authority, and request digest. Its `TimedLanguageAssetBundle@2` does not replace canonical words or hide adaptation. Source-script correction stales dependent editions.

Review factual support, quotation, attribution, privacy, likeness, disclosures, accessible language, and audience. Findings point to exact claims or text revisions. A bounded forward repair may answer them; unsupported statements or unresolved authority remain draft, correction request, or refusal.

[Edit](edit.md) receives the accepted script by digest. Later correction stales dependent narration, captions, cuts, renders, and publications, preserving the original words and review history.
