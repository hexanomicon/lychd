# :material-surround-sound: Picture-bound sound

Atelier judges the latch click, footsteps and room tone against the locked shot and mixes them
into the finished master. The production contract is [Picture-bound sound](../atelier/master.md#picture-bound-sound).

## Hear the scene, then admit the result

The producer auditions the sound against the exact picture, retains lineage and review, and
reopens the affected decision if the cut changes. A distributor can request a corrected mix
or different channel layout through [a new version](distribution.md#request-a-new-version).

## When the video model also returns sound

The producing owner and picture-sound brief must be declared before the attempt. The owner
then judges the returned sound with its compound parent and timebase. For new masters that owner
is Atelier revision `6`. Historical sound approvals from Broadcast revision `4` remain bound
to their original request and use. [Atelier's facet admission](../atelier/master.md#picture-bound-sound)
