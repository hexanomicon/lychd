# :material-timeline-text-outline: Edit

The audiovisual edit now belongs to [Atelier's video master](../atelier/master.md#edit-the-timeline).
Broadcast revision `4` kept the timeline inside its editorial package. Revision `5` receives the
finished cut to prepare it for distribution.

## Admit the assets

Atelier admits the exact visuals, music, timed language and picture sound into the cut, with
their producer judgments and use limits. Broadcast admits the resulting master and checks
whether a particular audience may receive it. [Timeline inputs](../atelier/master.md#edit-the-timeline)
· [Channel admission](distribution.md#prepare-the-channel-plan)

## Own the timeline

Atelier chooses placement, cuts, transitions and the final mix, then reviews the export.
A channel that needs a new crop or shorter ending sends its requirements and original digest
back to Atelier. [Request a new version](distribution.md#request-a-new-version)

For a one-clip operator exercise with a caption and optional narration, see
[Try a local cut](../atelier/short-video.md#try-a-local-cut).
