# :material-microphone-outline: Voice boundary

A sung phrase belongs to its arrangement; a later spoken line needs a different admission. Riffmaw owns singing, rap, musical spoken word, harmonies, and other vocal parts judged as music, without becoming a generic voice-pack or spoken-media owner.

Admit the take under approved lyrics, language/pronunciation assumptions, direction, musical role, timing, and acceptance criteria; `riffmaw.capture_take@2` returns `PerformanceTake@1` under those conditions. Through raw takes, comping, tuning, cleanup, generation, and tone changes, preserve parents, performer/model identity, controls, influence, cost when applicable, and digests. Review that history with the performance before accepted work enters `MusicAssetBundle@1`. A transcript can observe the performance but cannot certify its lyrics, pronunciation, or musical fit.

One take's consent authorizes neither cloning nor later words. Performer authority binds identity, lyrics, purpose, audience, territory, duration, transformations, disclosure, and revocation. Impersonation, concealed synthesis, unapproved words, or ambiguous authority returns review, replacement, or refusal.

[Language Edition](../language-edition/) owns dialogue, narration, spoken replacement, and captions within a source-aligned timed-language edition. [Echo](../../sepulcher/extensions/echo.md) retains speech capture/transcription/synthesis/cloning/delivery/playback facts. [Avatar](../avatar/index.md) may select an exact eligible vocal or acoustic-voice reference for a presentation without gaining new-word synthesis or publication permission.
