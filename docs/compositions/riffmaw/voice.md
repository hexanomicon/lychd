# :material-microphone-outline: Voice boundary

A sung phrase belongs to its arrangement; a later spoken line needs a different admission. Riffmaw owns singing, rap, musical spoken word, harmonies, and other vocal parts judged as music, without becoming a generic voice-pack or spoken-media owner.

`riffmaw.capture_take@2` returns `PerformanceTake@1` under approved lyrics, language/pronunciation assumptions, direction, musical role, timing, and acceptance criteria. Raw takes, comping, tuning, cleanup, generation, and tone changes preserve parents, performer/model identity, controls, influence, cost when applicable, and digests. Accepted work enters `MusicAssetBundle@1`. A transcript can observe a performance but cannot certify lyrics, pronunciation, or musical fit.

One take's consent authorizes neither cloning nor later words. Performer authority binds identity, lyrics, purpose, audience, territory, duration, transformations, disclosure, and revocation. Impersonation, concealed synthesis, unapproved words, or ambiguous authority returns review, replacement, or refusal.

[Language Edition](../language-edition/) owns ordinary dialogue, narration, spoken replacement, captions, and timed-language editions. [Echo](../../sepulcher/extensions/echo.md) retains speech capture/transcription/synthesis/cloning/delivery/playback facts. [Avatar](../avatar/index.md) may select an exact eligible vocal or acoustic-voice reference for a presentation without gaining new-word synthesis or publication permission.
