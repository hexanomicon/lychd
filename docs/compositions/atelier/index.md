# :material-camera-iris: Atelier

The Magus asks for a still image that can stand on its own. Once accepted, that image may later become the first frame of a moving sequence. Atelier carries either commission to its declared finish, keeping visual direction, source influence, candidates, and accepted revisions attributable.

The craft can combine generators, editors, renderers, procedural work, capture, and authored material. The Magus remains visual director. Here **VFX means visual effects**.

## The work

Start with [Brief](brief.md): what should exist when the commission is done, and how will you
judge it? [Direction](direction.md) turns those intentions into a visual language.

[Assets](assets.md) makes and reviews images, models, textures, and other visual roles.
Enter [Motion](motion.md) when the requested work changes through time. Once a result is
approved, [Package](package.md) seals the exact revisions another craft can admit.

## The requested finish

Choose the requested finish when admitting the commission: an accepted still, accepted motion, or an approved immutable `VisualAssetBundle@1`. The principal Pattern `atelier.build_visual_package@3`, published by `atelier.studio` revision `5`, works from that commission, frozen references, target profile, and acceptance criteria. If it cannot reach the requested boundary, it must return exact partial or non-completion under the pinned score.

Reaching a requested still boundary completes the Run; later motion begins a forward Invocation over the retained artifact. [Motion](motion.md#progressive-visual-depth) follows both motion within the pinned score and separately admitted continuation.

Atelier owns the commission, visual judgment, candidates, accepted revisions, provenance, and package. It stops before sound production, engine import, final audiovisual assembly, publication, or rights certification. [Riffmaw](../riffmaw/) owns music; [Language Edition](../language-edition/) owns timed-language editions; [Foundry](../foundry/) owns engine integration; [Broadcast](../broadcast/) owns picture sound, the final editorial relation, and publication.

These craft boundaries can meet in one proposed Atelier studio, with shared controls and a
timeline. [The studio example](../products-and-suites.md#atelier-riffmaw-language-edition-and-broadcast)
distinguishes that broader operator experience from this visual contract and follows joint
generation, music revisions, and game-directed work.
