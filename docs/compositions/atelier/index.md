# :material-camera-iris: Atelier

The Magus can commission a still, a moving sequence or a finished video. An illustrator may
stop at an accepted first frame. A video director may take that frame through motion, dialogue,
sound, edit and final review, then take the accepted master home.

## The work

In Altar, open **Applications → Atelier** for the Video master. The shot bin, program monitor
and timeline share a selection with the caption inspector. **Workflow** opens the current
recipe in Loom. [Use the cut and caption editor](../../divination/altar/atelier.md).

Start with [Brief](brief.md): what should exist when the commission is done, and how will you
judge it? [Direction](direction.md) turns those intentions into a visual language.

[Assets](assets.md) makes and reviews images, models, textures and other visual roles, including
visual effects (VFX). [Motion](motion.md) works with change across time. [Package](package.md)
seals accepted visual revisions for reuse.

For a finished video, [Video master](master.md) follows the storyboard, full timeline,
picture-bound sound, edit, mix, render and review. [A short video for a colleague](short-video.md)
follows a three-minute peer offer through both reviews and collection after a disconnect.
It includes a runnable local cut.

Follow a caption edit in a twelve-second illustrated story in the
[proposed Atelier cut workspace](../../divination/altar/applications.md#cut-a-video-in-atelier).

## The requested finish

`atelier.studio` revision `6` adds `atelier.finish_video_master@1`. Its named finish is an
accepted local `VideoMaster@1`. Atelier judges how everything meets in that master. [Riffmaw](../riffmaw/)
judges the music, and [Language Edition](../language-edition/) judges the timed-language edition.
A private return and a [Broadcast distribution](../broadcast/distribution.md) each require
authority for their own recipient, purpose and effect.

Revision `6` retains reusable visual Patterns under their published identities. The visual-only
`atelier.build_visual_package@3` and `atelier.forge_visual_sequence@3` keep their revision-`5`
commission and facet bindings. Their requested finish can be an accepted still, accepted
motion or an approved immutable `VisualAssetBundle@1`, according to the selected score.
Finishing a still completes that Run. New motion or a video master may admit the retained
artifact in a forward Invocation. A score that cannot reach its declared boundary returns the
exact partial result or non-completion.

[Foundry](../foundry/) judges engine import and playability. A Product may bring several crafts
into one studio, and a Suite can commission and await work from several live owners.
[The studio example](../products-and-suites.md#atelier-riffmaw-language-edition-and-broadcast)

## Revision continuity

??? info "Earlier designs and their exact successors"

    Revision `5` superseded revision `4`, advancing `atelier.build_visual_package` and
    `atelier.forge_visual_sequence` to `@3` with exact `ImageJob@2` and `VideoJob@2` Prism
    placements and pre-execution compound-facet authority. Revision `4` replaced
    `SyncCueMap@1` with `MusicCueMap@1`, routed returned sound by role, and advanced the
    former `@1` Patterns to `@2`. No Portfolio registry or Run used revisions `3` or `4`.
    Those historical references keep their meanings. The new master score has a separate
    identity and revision-`6` authority.
