# Professional Operations

Client casework needs attributable answers, prepared actions, and clear handoffs. Broker provides
the first route in this Area. Professional uses may also appear in other Areas, according to the
application's records and judgment.

| Composition | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| [Broker](../broker/index.md) | A client question answered from current offer knowledge, a prepared act, or a clear handoff or blocker. |
| [Creation investigation](../creation/index.md) | A public-source software question, exact analysis candidate and separately attributed verdict. |

The [Accounting Office candidate study](../accounting-office/index.md) explores attributable
business records, reconciliation, and accounting handoffs outside the accepted Portfolio.
