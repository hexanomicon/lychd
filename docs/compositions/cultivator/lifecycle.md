# Lifecycle

A perennial can enter another growing season without becoming a new subject. Cycle reviews
retain both the recurring season and the longer history that preceded it.

## Development and recurring cycles

`cultivator.review_cycle@1` produces `CultivatorCycle@1`, linking a culture,
its reviewed profile revision, evidence, and cycle history. Development, recurring
season, placement, and completed work are independent dimensions.

Annual, perennial, and cultivated fungi profiles may describe different cycles.
Fungi must not inherit botanically false plant stages. A perennial's recurring season
does not reset its identity or erase earlier development. Profile-qualified states
and transitions require evidence; calendar dates alone cannot advance a stage.

A newly reviewed profile does not silently rewrite earlier state claims. Record the
revision and evidence supporting each change, including corrections and unresolved
interpretations.

## Review boundaries

An observed symptom is distinct from an interpretation or expert finding. Missing
reports establish neither health nor death. Users may record suggestions and
professional guidance with their source; the composition claims no diagnostic,
pesticide approval, or food safety authority.

Each review is finite and may resolve to an evidence-supported change, refusal,
infeasibility, or unknown outcome. A growing season and a perennial's history remain
durable records across completed reviews; neither is an endless Run. Tending completion
and harvest receipts inform review without automatically proving a developmental
transition or end of life.
