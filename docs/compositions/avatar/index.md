# :material-account-outline: Avatar

One Lich can appear in a VR room while a Blockworld inhabitant continues elsewhere. Avatar keeps the presentation attributable across those places: which Persona and eligible form appeared, which projection was admitted, and what each target reported when it ended.

An eligible profile supplies the presentation envelope; a Morphe selects a form inside it. Separate target bindings carry the appearance through interruption and partial settlement. The Lich, its Persona, memory, and each target's effects remain with their owners.

## Contract

`avatar.presence` revision `2` publishes `avatar.compose_profile@2`, `avatar.bind_morphe@2`, and `avatar.project_presence@2`. The results are immutable `AvatarProfile@2`, `MorpheBinding@2`, `AvatarPresence@2`, and per-target `ProjectionBinding@2` records/receipts, or exact partial/non-completion. These contracts grant no identity change, unbounded ambient presence, target-world/device ownership, unobserved delivery/motion claim, or physical/virtual effect authority.
