# :material-tools: Maintenance

Maintenance turns attributable alarms, inspections, runtime, or calendar evidence into bounded
work. It can contain, investigate, schedule, and verify; it cannot manufacture professional
authority or close a fault from silence.

`homestead.maintain_site@1` returns containment advice, research needs, a work order, verification,
or an explicitly unresolved state. Requirements sent to [Scavenger](../scavenger/index.md) state
compatibility, certification, serviceability, environment, budget, and evidence needs. Scavenger
returns dated candidate and seller evidence, never purchase or commissioning authority.

A human or droid work order binds the exact asset, zone, tools, resources, expiry, stop conditions,
and expected verification. Design certification, permits, construction, unsafe energization,
interlock bypass, hazardous repair, and general robot authority remain outside Homestead. A local
controller or emergency stop may refuse fresh work even after LychD admitted it.

[Tether](../../sepulcher/extensions/tether.md) may eventually carry private inspection and exact
approved controls, but tunnel possession supplies no Principal or effect authority and never falls
back to a public dashboard. Cameras require a named purpose, privacy masks, active hours, viewers,
and retention; face recognition, neighbour surveillance, ambient audio, public streaming, and
indefinite retention are absent by default. Prism's future
[live Sight session](../../sepulcher/extensions/prism/sight.md) may analyze an admitted feed under
those limits; it gains no camera, maintenance, droid, or site authority.

A lost acknowledgement remains an unknown effect. Recovery reads the controller, independent
evidence, and any open work or hazard before repeat or closure. Return to
[Homestead](index.md).
