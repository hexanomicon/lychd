# :material-cart-outline: Provision

Provision replenishes ordinary household stores. Kaufland, Lidl, another merchant, a local market,
and a field are sources—not Composition identities and never ambient permission to browse,
authenticate, disclose, or buy.

## From need to result

`homestead.observe_shops@1` admits attributed, dated offers. Shop profiles are explicit and
independently revocable; public fetch, rendered observation, authenticated session, cart mutation,
address disclosure, order submission, and payment are separate powers and effects.

`homestead.plan_provision@1` combines household policy, stores, budgets, admitted offers, and an
optional purpose-limited `FoodNeed@1` from [Wellbeing](../wellbeing/eating.md) into a reviewable
replenishment plan. That need may contain quantities, declared exclusions, useful product traits,
expiry, privacy class, and unresolved flags—not diagnosis, journal, measurements, medication,
genetics, movement history, or merchant instructions.

`homestead.build_cart@1` creates a cart. `homestead.checkout@1` separately revalidates merchant,
product, package, price, stock, fees, delivery, substitutions, recurring terms, disclosure, and
worst-case budget before one authorized submission. It returns `ProvisionResult@1`: what became
available, unavailable, substituted, refused, or uncertain. That result makes no health, eating,
or adherence claim.

This office covers food, cleaning goods, toiletries, and other ordinary consumables entering
shared custody. Personal meal choice and preparation guidance stay with Wellbeing; once confirmed
cooking changes household lots, [Stores](stores.md) records the transformation. Eating out and
irregular, high-value, compatibility-heavy, property, or seller-negotiated acquisition stay
outside this office; Wellbeing keeps eating out and [Scavenger](../scavenger/index.md) keeps the
latter.

Offers, provision plans, carts, orders, deliveries, and reconciliation keep independent revisions.

Raw addresses, payment data, credentials, and one-time codes never enter prompts. A material cart
or checkout change returns to the Magus. Missing merchant acknowledgement becomes an unknown
checkout or payment result and closes retries until reconciliation establishes what happened.
Restart never duplicates cart mutation, order submission, notification, or delivery
reconciliation.
