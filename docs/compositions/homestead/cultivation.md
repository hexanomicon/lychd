# :material-sprout: Cultivation

Cultivation keeps plots, crops, soil, weather, water, work, and harvest connected without turning
agricultural observation into agronomic, pesticide, livestock, or food-safety authority.

`homestead.tend_land@1` combines attributed plot, crop, soil, weather, and resource state into an
explainable human or droid work order. A request to a [Legion](../../adr/42-legion.md) node carries
only a fenced task, zone, resources, expiry, and stop conditions—not shell access or a general farm
mandate. The body's freshest safety admission and emergency stop remain authoritative.

A confirmed harvest enters [Stores](stores.md) as a sourced lot with location, quantity interval,
condition uncertainty, and disposition. Harvest proves neither food safety nor consumption.
Water, energy, and equipment constraints remain in their owning ledgers, so one convenient crop
summary cannot hide a stale sensor, exhausted reserve, or unresolved hazard.
