# :material-sprout: Cultivation

Cultivation now belongs to [Cultivator](../cultivator/index.md): growing cultures, their spaces
and life cycles, tending judgment, and confirmed harvests. Continue with [Tending](../cultivator/tending.md)
for work planning or [Harvest](../cultivator/harvest.md) for collection and stock handoff.

This route preserves the boundary of retired Designed `homestead.steward` revision `1` and its
`homestead.tend_land@1` Pattern. The old Pattern is not an alias for Cultivator. Homestead keeps
physical site assets, resources, commissioned controls, and separate acceptance of collected
material into stores. [Revision continuity](index.md#revision-continuity) records the successor
and absence of an executable migration.
