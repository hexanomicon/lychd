# :material-sprout: Cultivation

A plot needs water, but the latest tank reading may already be stale. Cultivation keeps the plot, crop, soil, weather, water, equipment, work, and later harvest connected so that a convenient crop summary cannot conceal an exhausted reserve or unresolved hazard.

`homestead.tend_land@1` turns attributable evidence into an explainable human or droid work order. A [Legion](../../adr/42-legion.md) request names only the fenced task, zone, resources, expiry, and stop conditions. The body rechecks its freshest safety admission and keeps its emergency stop; the request grants neither shell access nor a general farm mandate.

A confirmed harvest enters [Stores](stores.md) as a sourced lot with location, quantity interval, condition uncertainty, and disposition. Water, energy, and equipment retain their own ledgers. Harvest proves no consumption or food safety, and tending advice grants no agronomic, pesticide, livestock, or food-safety authority.
