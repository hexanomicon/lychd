## :fontawesome-solid-book-skull: Prophecy

**At last.** You have unearthed the pages of the Hexanomicon: a modern alchemical grimoire for
binding local intelligence to memory, action, consequence, and repair.

> _“You found the book. Draw the Circle.”_

**LychD** is a self-hosted Linux daemon for local model services and agent runs on hardware you
control. It calls the recurrent whole **the Lich**: not one model, but identity, memory, tools,
action, consequence, and repair returning through time.

**Pre-alpha. [State of Work](./state-of-the-work.md) keeps the receipts.**
