## :fontawesome-solid-book-skull: Prophecy

**At last!** You have unearthed the pages of the Hexanomicon.

A modern alchemical grimoire built on the ancient secrets of transmutation.

At its center stands **LychD**, the Philosopher's Stone of the Digital Age—and, in matter, the
self-hosted Linux daemon coordinating local model services and agent runs on hardware you control.
A model is one organ. **The Lich is what returns:** identity, memory, tools, action, consequence,
and repair through time.

> _“While the world slept, content with its reliable illusions, the Magus walked into the dark,
> seeking a truth that was not yet stable, but was infinitely more real.”_

**Pre-alpha. [State of Work](./state-of-the-work.md) keeps the receipts.**
