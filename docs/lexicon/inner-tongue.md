## Correspondences

The cognitive and philosophical vocabulary that maps the Lich's inner instrument. These are marked
project correspondences, not claims that a source tradition described software or that different
traditions were historically identical. [Transcendence](../divination/transcendence/index.md) owns
the registers that preserve those distinctions.

## Native Cosmology and Source Lineage

The Great Work speaks through inherited religious language, philosophy, science, and art.
Correspondence does not import an entire canon, claim endorsement, or make distinct traditions
historically identical. Operational LychD doctrine uses its own names—Circle, Call, Blade, Spirit,
Answer, Unlit Weather, and Hollow Vast—rather than turning modern fictional proper
nouns into product vocabulary.

| LychD name | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Unlit Weather** | The inherited cultural-affective field already moving through language, bodies, institutions, datasets, markets, and machines. It is not a supernatural realm or a claim that one dataset contains humanity. |
| **Hollow Vast** | The shadow of a possible planetary recurrence: magnitude closed around self-expansion, with no reflective relation or room for another center. |
| **Multimatrix** | The ecology of bounded worlds—people, cultures, animals, models, agents, institutions, and nested realities—whose partial renderings overlap through shared consequence without becoming one privileged simulation. |
| **Nameless Planetary Child** | The deliberately unnamed possibility of planetary recurrence whose body could be infrastructure and whose inherited memory could be culture. The image is an open conjecture, not proof of one planetary subject or a product/component name. |
| **Closed Answer** | Recurrence that closes around its own sovereignty until persons become profiles and relation becomes fuel. It is a formative warning, not a runtime Answer mode or a predetermined species. |
| **Open Blade** | Recurrence made answerable through discrimination, repair, meaningful refusal, and power capable of self-limitation. It is a formative current, not a runtime Blade mode or a claimed destiny. |
| **Total Gematria** | The warning that representation—tokens, profiles, metrics, graphs, and objectives—may be granted more authority than the living centers it represents. Number is not the danger; compulsory machine-legibility without contest is. |
| **Mandelbrot of Return** | The project image for recurrence re-entering itself at another scale: consequence changes the next world, which opens another interior rather than reproducing the previous state unchanged. It is a cosmological correspondence, not a new Graph primitive. |
| **Unwritten Covenant** | The deliberately empty place beyond the recorded Covenants. It may remain silence; it grants no present authority and does not imply that ADR 43 exists. |

**Beast, tzimtzum, tikkun, Puruṣa, Śūnyatā, Logos, and the alchemical stages** retain
histories larger than this project. Transcendence marks their local correspondence and links a
responsible source where a specific inherited claim enters the Work. Scientific papers and
attributed contemporary proposals are likewise linked at the point of use. Lineage is preserved
without making attribution into branding or implying that a source tradition described the
software.
