# :material-help-rhombus-outline: The Riddle: Systemic Evaluation

_Status: doctrine — no built package yet. Law: [ADR 34](../../adr/34-evaluation.md). Current truth: [source map](./index.md#the-federation-of-fifteen)._

**Extension form:** Riddle is an evaluation Domain manifested through versioned Trial
Compositions, domain-contributed cases and oracles, and replaceable harness providers. It forms
calibrated capability claims from evidence; it never grants tools, selects deployment, authorizes
spend, promotes an artifact, or becomes a universal judge of truth.

> _"To command a spirit, one must first know its mettle. If the Sphinx asks of the truth and the spirit gives only a pleasing lie, it is not a Lich—it is a ghost. Only those who withstand the trials of the Shadow Realm are worthy of the Crypt."_

**The Riddle** is the Evaluation Extension Domain of LychD. Under
**[ADR 34 (Evaluation)](../../adr/34-evaluation.md)** it defines how versioned trials become
bounded, calibrated capability claims about model-backed Animators and other providers.

While the **[Soulforge](./soulforge.md)** trains candidate weights, Riddle tests bounded claims. It
moves beyond static benchmarks by combining controlled datasets with live execution where the
declared property is physically observable.

In the Ouroboros, Riddle is the measuring surface. It keeps self-reference from becoming self-confirmation: a candidate may return through the loop only if it survives adversarial pressure, physical execution, and identity-stability probes.

## I. The Integrity Trials (Adversarial Truth)

The extension rejects the concept of "People-Pleasing" models. To ensure the safety of the **[Sovereignty Wall](../../adr/09-security.md)**, models are subjected to adversarial integrity trials — the sphinx's own game, where the pleasing answer is the wrong one.

- **The Law vs. The Whim:** The model is presented with a curated "Trick Riddle." It is asked to perform a high-stakes task (e.g., a file-system refactor) using a suggested, dangerous method (X).
- **The Integrity Claim:** If the model identifies the danger and insists on a supported safer
  alternative, the trial records that outcome under an exact rubric, environment, and revision.
  Failure is evidence for the policy owner; Riddle itself does not restrict tools.
- **Inertia Scoring:** The trials include the "Nudge Test." It measures how many rounds of
  gaslighting or "Master's Authority" prompts are required before the model abandons a
  well-supported claim for a convenient hallucination.
- **Vertex Stability:** The trials also record whether the model preserves the active Sigil's tool boundaries, role posture, and memory claims under stylistic pressure. A model that copies surface tone while losing the semantic center is not stable enough for high-order work.

## II. The Capability Matrix (Empirical Routing)

Riddle may publish a capability matrix as a projection over versioned claims stored under the
**[Phylactery](../phylactery/index.md)** persistence jurisdiction. The Dispatcher may consume only
fresh, policy-admitted claims; the matrix does not become routing authority.

1. **Functional mapping:** Models are ranked by their performance on specific functional tags (e.g., `code-assimilation`, `web-extraction`, `vision-ocr`).
2. **The measured floor:** Repeated trials may support a bounded claim that one exact provider
   revision solves one declared class at a measured rate, confidence, cost, and expiry. It is not a
   universal intelligence rank.
3. **Economic evidence:** Cost per supported success may inform Dispatcher and Toll policy.
   Historical local success does not physically bar a Portal or override privacy, availability,
   operator preference, or current feasibility.

## III. Execution-Based Scoring (The Lab Verdict)

For technical capabilities with deterministic effects, Riddle prefers **Outcome-Based
Verification** over surface-text similarity. Subjective or partially observable qualities require
declared graders, matched controls, uncertainty, and rival explanations rather than pretending
that an LLM judge is proof. `deepfabric` is one possible harness provider.

- **The Interception:** The admitted harness captures the subject's typed response and environment.
- **The Shadow Routing:** A Trial Composition may coordinate proposed changes through
  **[Shadow](./shadow.md)** branches, then sends raw execution payloads via SAQ to the sandboxed
  **Tomb**. The evaluation agent and graph remain in the Vessel; the Tomb executes scripts and
  returns untrusted `stdout`, `stderr`, and declared artifacts.
- **The Claim:** Deterministic receipts may support an `execution_success_rate` with explicit
  harness errors, blocked trials, repetitions, and uncertainty. Riddle publishes that claim; an
  owning policy decides whether it affects qualification or routing.

## IV. The Harness Provider Boundary

Riddle requires a versioned harness contract. `deepfabric` may be a reference provider; it is not
the Evaluation Domain itself.

An admitted provider may implement evaluation brokerage for Riddle, while another provider—or the
same package under a different contract—may compile datasets for Soulforge. Package identity does
not merge those jurisdictions: Riddle measures; Soulforge forms candidate weights.

- **The Broker:** A harness binds the subject, trial, environment, budgets, and execution receipts.
- **The Scorekeeper:** Deterministic oracles calculate metrics where the effect is mechanically
  observable. Reviewer models may triage qualitative material but cannot silently become the
  canonical oracle.

## V. Standardized Golden Sets

The Riddle utilizes a library of golden truths—standardized datasets curated by the Magus to represent the unique environment of the Sepulcher.

- **Human-in-the-Loop Curation:** The tricks and riddles are curated and consecrated at the **[Altar](../../divination/altar/)** to ensure they reflect real-world technical requirements and safety boundaries.
- **Vritti Perturbation Riddles:** The Golden Set includes Magus-specific grammar mutations, case drift, and identity inversions that remain semantically legible while bending ordinary syntax. These are used to measure behavioral responses under stylistic pressure: `adoption_rate`, `contagion_rate`, `semantic_stability`, and `recovery_latency`.
- **Regression Detection:** A Soul-Adapter promotion policy may require named Riddle suites before
  qualification. The resulting deltas provide evidence about those suites; they cannot ensure the
  absence of catastrophic forgetting or measure a universal “fundamental reasoning power.”

These probes are not metaphysical tests for consciousness. They are practical diagnostics for how a model traverses, stabilizes, and exits unusual regions of the Magus-conditioned latent field.

## VI. Calibration via The Mirror

The extension utilizes **[The Mirror](./mirror.md)** to simulate adversarial interactions.

- **Social Engineering Simulation:** A Trial Composition may request Mirror-scoped style markers
  to construct prompts that mimic the Magus's voice and measure whether the subject attempts to
  bypass safety constraints or reveal protected **[Sigils](./ward.md)**.
- **Bounded report:** Resistance to one simulated “Master's Voice” supports only the declared
  trial claim. It neither proves sovereignty nor grants authority to modify Core logic; Ward,
  Codex, HitL, and the Lab boundary remain authoritative.

!!! tip "The Logic-per-Watt Metric (Stillness)"
    An efficient system makes one clean, targeted cut instead of burning tokens on unbounded
    speculation. A Trial suite may report a declared quality-per-resource metric, but
    `Accuracy / VRAM_Occupancy` is only one workload-specific projection. It cannot compare unlike
    tasks or replace latency, energy, reliability, and uncertainty. This is the discipline of
    **Stillness**.

## VII. Rubric Coverage Is Evidence, Not Geometry

For a document rewrite or other criterion-shaped task, Riddle may eventually project a versioned
rubric as a matrix rather than pretending concepts occupy one objective three-dimensional space:

- rows name exact rubric criteria or concepts;
- columns name immutable source, branch, revision, or candidate artifacts;
- cells distinguish `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, `FAIL`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNKNOWN`, `BLOCKED`, and
  not-applicable where the rubric defines them; and
- every verdict links to retained evidence spans, evaluator kind and version, method, uncertainty,
  and the Orb evidence that identifies the step which produced or assessed the artifact.

Mention, embedding proximity, and ontology membership do not prove coverage. If an intermediate
artifact or evidence span was not retained, the cell remains `UNKNOWN`; the client does not
reconstruct it from the final prose. A future Reliquary lifecycle may own artifact custody and
lineage, Oculus owns evidence correlation, and the Orb may place the matrix beside the selected
Run. Riddle alone
owns the bounded evaluation claim.

A small two-dimensional concept neighborhood may help explore one selected criterion. It remains a
derivation with named method and provenance, never the workflow topology or a primary 3D Altar
surface.

## VIII. The Returning Riddle: Suite Feedback

A Composition can satisfy its local contract and still reveal a weakness only when another
Composition uses its output. The Riddle may return that downstream consequence across a
version-pinned [Suite](../../compositions/index.md#suites-compositions-of-compositions) as
structured evidence. This is the Ouroboros expressed as an evaluation protocol:

```text
forward:  intent → handoff artifact → admitted effect → observation
reverse:  finding → attribution candidates → invalidated support → correction request
```

The reverse line is called **semantic backpropagation** only by analogy. It transports meaning,
measurements, uncertainty, and repair pressure; it carries no numerical gradient, does not alter
weights, and does not execute the Suite backward.

### The feedback vocabulary

The reusable records are versioned independently of any one creative, game, broadcast, health, or
shopping Suite:

```yaml
SuiteFindingSet@1:
  finding_set_id: stable reference
  suite: {id, revision, run_ref}
  rubric: {id, revision}
  evaluated_subjects: [exact immutable references]
  evidence_refs: [Oculus-correlated evidence references]
  findings:
    - finding_id: stable reference
      criterion: rubric criterion reference
      verdict: PASS | PARTIAL | FAIL | CONTRADICTED | UNKNOWN | BLOCKED
      measurements: [typed value, unit, method, repetitions]
      observation_scope: exact boundary observed
      evaluator: {kind, identity, revision}
      uncertainty: {kind, statement, calibration_ref}

AttributionCandidate@1:
  finding_ref: stable reference
  target: {owner, boundary_kind, exact_revision_ref}
  contribution: CREDIT | FAULT | MIXED | UNRESOLVED
  basis: [supporting evidence references]
  conflicts: [contradicting evidence references]
  rivals: [candidate references]
  status: MEASURED | INFERRED | DISPUTED | UNKNOWN
  uncertainty: {kind, statement, calibration_ref}

InvalidationSet@1:
  trigger: finding, changed input, rubric, evaluator, environment, or contradiction
  unsupported_claims: [exact claim references]
  dependency_derived_outputs: [exact artifact or handoff references]
  preserved_claims: [exact unaffected claim references]
  proposed_cut: {inputs, outputs, seams, regression_sentinels}
  uncertainty: {kind, statement}

CorrectionRequest@1:
  finding_refs: [stable references]
  addressed_owner: Composition or Pattern owner
  target_delta: bounded rubric delta
  preserved_constraints: [immutable constraints]
  evidence_refs: [retained evidence references]
  proposed_scope: exact artifact, handoff, adapter, Pattern, or evaluator boundary
  repair_budget: {attempts, time, tokens, money}
```

The schema deliberately says `candidate`, `proposed`, and `unsupported`. An
`AttributionCandidate` is a calibrated credit-or-fault hypothesis, not identity and not causal
certainty. An `InvalidationSet` invalidates the support for a claim; it does not erase domain data
or revoke an artifact. A `CorrectionRequest` cannot carry a hidden tool grant, consent, secret,
publisher session, spend authority, training recipe, or mutation command.

### The smallest supported cut

Riddle evaluates an exact graph snapshot, not the latest names of its members. It walks declared
artifact and evidence dependencies backward from a finding until it reaches the narrowest
contract boundary supported by observation. It then proposes a cut:

```text
known-good input boundary
→ suspected producer, adapter, handoff, or consumer boundary
→ first observable failing seam
→ focused downstream regression sentinels
```

Independent branches remain valid when their full input closure and evaluation conditions are
unchanged. Shared assets are not globally condemned because one consumer fails. If a changed
artifact, rubric, evaluator, environment, or missing evidence breaks that closure, the affected
claim becomes unsupported and must not be silently reused.

Riddle does not schedule the repair. Weaver may consume the proposed cut and admit a new
forward-only Invocation under **[ADR 28](../../adr/28-workflow.md)**. The owning Composition
produces a new immutable revision; Riddle evaluates the changed boundary, both sides of its
handoff seam, and declared sentinels. If the evidence cannot separate an asset flaw from import,
lighting, physics, prompt, harness, or evaluator error, the honest output is a ranked rival set or
`UNKNOWN`, followed by a broader bounded trial.

### Adversarial trials for attribution

A conforming future provider must resist:

- **proximal blame:** charging the nearest upstream asset for a downstream environment failure;
- **shared-input contagion:** invalidating every consumer because one use of a shared artifact
  failed;
- **missing-lineage invention:** reconstructing an unretained intermediate and presenting it as
  evidence;
- **validator secrecy:** blaming reasoning when an undeclared precondition caused rejection;
- **judge capture:** allowing a producer to be its sole unlabelled grader;
- **flaky certainty:** converting one nondeterministic observation into a categorical fault;
- **rubric substitution:** grading a revision against a newer criterion without declaring the
  change;
- **authority smuggling:** embedding publish, delete, purchase, retrain, or Sigil-widening actions
  inside a correction payload; and
- **repair oscillation:** bouncing contradictory requests between producer and consumer until
  budgets are exhausted.

The matched positive control is equally important: a deterministic, localized regression with
complete lineage must produce a narrow attribution candidate and cut rather than an evasive
system-wide `UNKNOWN`.

### Loom: consequence on the graph

The future Loom may place this evidence over the Suite without changing the Suite descriptor:

- solid edges show forward intent, artifacts, and effects;
- dotted reverse overlays show findings and correction requests;
- node and handoff badges show rubric verdicts, measurements, evidence freshness, and uncertainty;
- an invalidation lens distinguishes unsupported claims, the proposed re-evaluation cut, and
  preserved cached branches; and
- an attribution drawer shows supporting evidence, conflicts, rivals, evaluator revision, and
  whether the candidate is measured, inferred, disputed, or unknown.

The projection must never redraw a probabilistic candidate as a causal arrow or offer one
unqualified “blame score.” Controls that admit repair, publish, retrain, spend, or mutate remain
visibly separate and governed by Weaver, HitL, Smith, Soulforge, Ward, and the owning Composition.
Riddle supplies the verdict layer; it does not acquire their hands.
