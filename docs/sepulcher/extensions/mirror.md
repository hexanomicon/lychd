# :material-mirror: Mirror Identity

A temporary Agent finishes its step; the act outlasts it. **Mirror** keeps the act and its
contributors bound to an operative identity, so a later reader can ask who spoke, under whose
authority, and through which revision. Every admitted Invocation records an exact Persona revision
or an explicit no-Persona choice under [Identity](../../adr/32-identity.md).

Mirror remains **Designed**. The fixed `The First One` AgentSpec, static identity instruction,
and loopback `magus:*` Sigil demonstrate current Agent and authority plumbing. They do not yet
supply revisioned Personas or reproducible hydration; [State](../../state-of-the-work.md#mirror-identity)
keeps that delivery boundary.

## The mirror and the Mirror

The lowercase [*mirror*](../../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#the-ego) is the contemplative image of mind.
Capitalized **Mirror** keeps Persona lineage and attribution in their technical home.

## I. The Answer / Ahaṃkāra {#i-identity-as-simulation-the-answer-ahamkara}

[Answer](../lich/answer.md) binds the focal “I.” Answer and [The EGO](../../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#the-ego)
develop identity, attachment, and Corruption. Mirror records the exact identity binding through
which an act becomes attributable.

## II. The Names in the Glass

Begin with the request: the **Principal** initiated or approved it, and the active **Sigil**
records the bounded authority. The principal may differ from the human or machine **producer**
that contributed the result. Asking for work does not make every contribution one's own.

Then identify the performance. The **Agent** is one typed cognitive step; **AgentSpec** defines
its executable contract, and **Posture** constrains the particular act. Pattern and Invocation
identify the admitted workflow performance; **Run** supplies its durable execution identity.
[Agents](../../adr/20-agents.md) owns Agent and Posture semantics;
[Spellweaver](../../adr/28-workflow.md) owns Pattern and Invocation law. The
[identity distinctions](../../adr/32-identity.md#keep-the-names-apart) keep these bindings exact.

An act may also be shaped by a **Persona**: a durable, revisioned voice, commitments, boundaries
and orientation. It is neither the caller nor the producer nor the temporary step. Here voice
means discourse and manner; acoustic voice, music, timed-language editions and presentation assets
retain their separate owners. Eligible identity-scoped memory may enter hydration.

## III. Attribution Without Laundering

Follow an artifact through two applications. Its first attribution envelope distinguishes the
initiating and approving principals from delegate or actor, Persona, AgentSpec, Posture, Sigil,
exact workflow revisions, human and machine producers, sources, tools, and handoffs. When a second
application consumes it, the original producer remains on the artifact. The consumer opens its
own separately admitted Invocation and binding. Selection cannot rewrite origin: human edits
and rejected branches retain their authorship as well.

A **spotless** Mirror keeps provenance clear enough to show the stain: exact origins and
revisions, ruptures, contradictions, corrections, and rejected branches remain visible. Clarity
never requires erasing a difficult part of the lineage.

Attribution alone establishes no authorization, truth, quality, credit, or fault.
[Ward](./ward.md) owns authentication and authorization; [Drift](./drift/index.md) supplies evaluated
findings. Identity continuity rests on exact revision lineage and reproducible binding. An
enduring process, generic workflow continuity, memory, resemblance, or a reputation for trust
cannot substitute for them.

## IV. Summoning and Hydration {#v-summoning-and-hydration}

To prepare an Agent, hydration takes the Persona revision pinned by the admitted Pattern—or its
explicit no-Persona choice—and combines it with Pattern and AgentSpec requirements, active
Principal and Sigil, and eligible identity-scoped memory. It produces ordered identity Context
within bounds and a receipt recording that construction. Fresh and resumed Agents keep the
pinned revision. Resemblance to yesterday's voice cannot supply the missing binding.
