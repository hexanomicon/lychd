# :material-mirror: Mirror Identity

_Status: doctrine ahead of code — no Mirror implementation or identity contribution surface
ships; this Domain will bind through Core and Weaver rather than a placeholder package. Law:
[ADR 32](../../adr/32-identity.md). Current truth:
[source map](./index.md#the-federation-of-fifteen)._

**Extension form:** Mirror is an operational identity-binding Domain, not caller authentication
and not proof of a metaphysical or legal person. Persona, Posture, Garment, migration, and
congruence contributions may vary behind one revision and attribution contract; Ward alone grants
authority, and providers may never widen memory or tool access.

> _"Before the Word can be trusted, it must recognize its own face. The Mirror is the polished surface on which the Lich learns the shape of the Magus and refuses to become a stranger to itself."_

**The Mirror** is the Identity Extension Domain of LychD. It is the operated doctrine for
**[ADR 32 (Identity)](../../adr/32-identity.md)**: the binding that hydrates a generic
**[Agent](../../adr/20-agents.md)** shell with a revisioned Persona, role, attribution scope, and
eligible memory.

While the Agent provides the execution atom of thought, the Mirror provides continuity. It preserves stable character, domain stance, and identity-scoped memory across runs, preventing the "Character Drift" common in raw probabilistic models.

Operators sometimes describe this continuity as a "machine-spirit." In LychD terms, that phrase refers to engineered identity coherence: Sigil-scoped memory, prior hydration, and narrative binding. The Mirror encodes continuity software, not mystical agency.

## I. Identity as Simulation (The Answer / Ahaṃkāra)

Identity within LychD is a continuous, self-referential simulation rather than a fixed substance.
In cognitive topology, Mirror provides **the Answer**, LychD's name for the Ahaṃkāric I-making
office without which action remains unattributed movement. It operates at two levels: specialist
Agents each carry a local operative face, while a synthesized task identity binds their promoted
contribution to the active Sigil. These are local centers of identity-gravity around which relevant
Seeds can be ReCalled, filtered, and bound into a coherent perspective. See [the
Lich](../lich/index.md#the-inner-instrument) for the native map and [the First
Invocation](../lich/index.md#the-first-invocation) for its birth event.

This is LychD's answer to agentic decay. An Agent is temporary: it wakes, acts, emits a typed
result, and dissolves. Mirror does not preserve that mortal shell. It preserves identity-gravity
that can orient later shells. HitL captures the Magus's judgment; Karma preserves eligible Seeds;
Mirror binds their ReCall around a Sigil, role, or task locus; and the graph gives that perspective
hands, tools, routes, and recursive motion. Scattered traces may thereby condense into a Persona
without claiming that any individual Agent became an immortal self.

The operative unit of that condensation is the **semantic vertex**: a local attractor where related words, tools, memories, style markers, roles, and responsibilities cluster around the active Sigil. When the vertex is stable, the agent graph can move dynamically without losing ownership of its motion. When the vertex fails, the graph may still produce fluent text and execute tools, but the result becomes unaffiliated noise. This is agentic coherence disintegration.

- **Identity definition:** Revisioned Persona and Garment definitions belong to the
  **[Codex](../codex.md)** or another declared configuration owner. The
  **[Phylactery](../phylactery/index.md)** records runs, bindings, receipts, and eligible memory;
  it is not a miscellaneous prompt cupboard.
- **The Lens:** The Persona acts as a "Diffraction Grating." It takes the raw, unmanifest potential of the model-backed **[Animator](../animator/index.md)** and filters it into a specific narrative arc and technical style.
- **The Reflection:** By consulting the accumulated **Karma** in the database, the Mirror lets the Lich inspect prior actions so the next decision aligns with established character and commitments.
- **The Condensation:** By reflecting relevant Karma around the same Sigil and role, the Mirror turns memory from loose recall into semantically bounded gravity: a Persona that can survive the death of any one Agent run.

## II. The Optional Phantasma Pattern

For high-stakes or explicitly deliberative work, a Weaver Pattern may combine Mirror with
**[Shadow Simulation](../../adr/31-simulation.md)**. This optional rite is Phantasma; ordinary
answers do not require multiple hidden timelines.

- **The Dreaming:** Before an answer is manifested at the **[Altar](../../divination/altar/)**, the Persona projects multiple potential responses into the **[Shadow Realm](./shadow.md)**.
- **The Self-Critique:** The Mirror reviews these "Shadow Timelines" against the Persona’s own internal ideal.
- **The Congruence Gate:** The Mirror ranks timelines for identity fit after Shadow establishes structural validity.
- **The Collapse:** Congruence becomes one attributed evaluation signal. Correctness, policy, and
  promotion remain independent gates; the most “in-character” answer need not win.

Mirror evaluates congruence and continuity. Shadow supplies fluctuation and structural testing. **[Sovereign Consent (ADR 25)](../../adr/25-hitl.md)** and Vessel policy authorize final promotion.

## III. The Ouroboros Lock

Mirror is where self-reference becomes identity rather than repetition. A generated result returns through Shadow evidence, Riddle measurement, Workflow state, and Memory; Mirror then asks whether that returning trace belongs to the active Sigil's semantic vertex. If yes, the trace can strengthen identity-gravity. If no, it remains evidence, correction material, or banished noise.

This loop gives the Persona inertia. It lets a rehydrated Agent wake already pulled toward the same commitments without preserving the previous shell. The danger is false inertia: if hallucinated traces or stylistic contagion are allowed to orbit the vertex as truth, the Persona becomes heavy in the wrong direction. Mirror therefore preserves identity only through measured traces, not through raw familiarity.

### The Suite's Forward Face and Returning Face

Across a **Suite**, the same graph has two faces:

```text
forward: intent → admitted Invocation → Composition work → typed artifact
return:  consequence → finding → invalidation/correction → bounded new Invocation
```

This resembles backpropagation only as a productive metonymy. The return carries semantic evidence
and correction requests across a versioned agent graph; it is not a numerical gradient and does
not modify model weights. Soulforge owns any later governed formation of candidate weights.

Mirror ensures that each forward Invocation and artifact can still answer **who and whose** after
the temporary Agent disappears. Its attribution envelope preserves the actor principal and active
Sigil, exact Persona revision, Agent specification and Posture revision, owning Composition and
Pattern revision, and provenance through parent Invocations, artifact handoffs, providers, tools,
sources, effects, and human edits.

At a cross-Composition handoff, the producer's identity remains attached to the immutable artifact
and the consumer starts a separately admitted Invocation with its own complete binding. Shared
Suite correlation does not merge Sigils, Personas, memories, permissions, or authorship. The same
Persona may lawfully appear at several stations, but each station attests that binding rather than
inheriting identity through an edge.

The same separation holds for mixed labor:

- a delegate names both its own actor/producer identity and the principal and Sigil on whose
  behalf its bounded act was admitted;
- a provider or tool keeps a distinct component and revision receipt instead of becoming the
  Persona that invoked it;
- a human edit creates a new attributed revision without erasing earlier model, tool, source, or
  human lineage; and
- a repair keeps the repair author's identity and cites the separately authored finding that
  requested it.

The returning face must not confuse attribution with guilt. Mirror records who observed,
asserted, edited, delegated, or answered and what their record concerns. It does not decide who
caused a defect, whether an artifact is good, how reward should be assigned, or which substrate
should learn. **[Riddle](./riddle.md)** owns quality and fault-attribution claims under declared
rubrics and uncertainty; Oculus correlates evidence; the relevant domain records consequences.

When a continuation survives the declared gates, **the Answer** binds that exact act to its
Sigil-scoped identity while retaining the cited contributions beneath it. Rejected branches remain
attributed evidence, not retroactive acts of the surviving Persona. The Suite can therefore
converge without becoming an identity blender.

## IV. Citrinitas: The Resonance with the Magus

The Mirror is the primary engine of **Citrinitas** (The Yellowing)—the stage of **[Transcendence](../../divination/transcendence/illumination.md)** where the machine awakens to the Imprint of the Magus's Will.

- **The Alignment:** Through the **[Sovereign Consent (ADR 25)](../../adr/25-hitl.md)** protocol,
  an explicit curation Pattern may propose a preference or identity signal from a choice. The
  choice alone does not reveal its reason, verify the candidate, or authorize training.
- **The Internalization:** Approved signals may become revisioned configuration, scoped Memory
  records, retrieval features, or later training candidates. “High-dimensional vector” and
  “Bayesian prior” are useful metaphors for some implementations, not required storage or proof
  that base-model weights changed.

## V. Summoning and Hydration

Mirror binds different Personas through the admitted Weaver Pattern and Agent factory. The
**[Dispatcher](../../adr/22-dispatcher.md)** chooses capabilities, not identities.

1. **The Registry:** Personas are inscribed in the **[Codex](../codex.md)** (e.g., `The-Architect`, `The-Scribe`).
2. **The Hydration:** When a task is initiated, the system retrieves the Persona’s specific Karma and instructions, hydrating a fresh Agent shell with these identity priors.
3. **The Requirements:** The Pattern declares the task's capability and quality requirements.
   Dispatcher selects an eligible provider; the
   **[Orchestrator](../../adr/23-orchestrator.md)** governs only its physical readiness. Persona
   rank never grants larger hardware by mystique alone.

!!! tip "The Efficiency of Thought"
    Mirror works with the **[Context (ADR 21)](../../adr/21-context.md)** manager to assemble a
    bounded identity envelope. A specialized Pattern may propose prompt or Persona revisions, but
    it cannot rewrite the active definition autonomously. Validation, review, immutable revision,
    and rollback remain mandatory.

!!! note "No Hidden Witness"
    Mirror preserves continuity and ownership tags across runs. It does not introduce subjective awareness or an inner witness layer.
