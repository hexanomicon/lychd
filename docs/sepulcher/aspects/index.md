# :material-hubspot: Aspects

> _Many arts gather beneath one skin._

Search the web, judge an artifact, train a model candidate, carry a voice: different work needs
different responsibilities. **Aspects** names that map of the body. Follow an Aspect to understand
its work, the decisions it owns, and its cooperation with the rest of LychD.

## Find the responsibility

| Aspect | Work it owns |
| --- | --- |
| [Oculus](oculus.md) | **Evidence and observability:** follow observations, correlations, derivations, and explicit gaps back to their sources. |
| [Tether](tether.md) | **Private reachability:** open exact private paths through the accepted WireGuard transport. |
| [Veil](veil.md) | **Hostile ingress:** terminate transport, canonicalize metadata, and admit declared routes. |
| [Ward](ward.md) | **IAM and authority:** resolve principals and recheck current permission over an exact object or effect. |
| [Spellweaver](weaver/index.md) | **Workflow:** validate Spell contracts and Scroll lifecycle, admit castings, and preserve continuity and logical time. Its methods include [Smith](weaver/smith.md) for software work and [Drift](weaver/drift/index.md) for evaluation. |
| [Webcrawler](webcrawler.md) | **Web acquisition:** bound search, fetch, extraction, rendering, and interaction. |
| [Soulforge](soulforge/index.md) | **Training:** bind admitted corpus, base digest, objective, Recipe, Training Run, evaluation, and candidate-weight lineage. |
| [Toll](toll.md) | **Economics:** distinguish quote, authorization, settlement, delivery, refund, and reconciliation. |
| [Prism](prism/index.md) | **Visual and spatial grounding:** bind observations, transformations, reconstructions, and generated forms to exact sources and derivatives. |
| [Echo](echo.md) | **Speech lifecycle:** carry capture, transcription, synthesis, and delivery with origin, consent, and outcome intact. |
| [Shadow](shadow/index.md) | **Possibility lineage:** hold isolated candidate worlds with exact parentage, evidence, and terminal disposition. |
| [Mirror](mirror.md) | **Identity:** preserve Persona lineage, hydration provenance, and attribution. |
| [Legion](legion.md) | **Distributed embodiment:** fence delegation across operator-owned bodies while each retains authority over its iron. |

This map is open-ended. An Aspect may be realized by native code, a working method, or several
cooperating services. Its name does not require a separate process, package, or interchangeable
implementation. [State of Work](../../state-of-the-work.md) records which parts can be used today.

## Follow a change across the body

Webcrawler may acquire a source for a proposed software change. [Smith](weaver/smith.md) carries the
change through inquiry, construction, verification, and a request for delivery. When it adopts
foreign craft, [Assimilation](weaver/assimilation.md) preserves provenance, rights, and local
maintenance obligations. Shadow may hold an isolated alternative and Drift may evaluate it.
The receiving owner still decides whether to admit the resulting effect.

At the edges, a private path or admitted route leaves application authority with the service
behind it. Oculus observes records without replacing their owners, and Mirror attributes identity
without authorizing the caller. The contracts make cooperation possible without merging those
decisions.

To add a capability or mechanism, use [Extensions](../extensions/index.md). That guide follows
concrete contributions—Runes, Spells, services, routes, and the other accepted integration paths.
[ADR 05](../../adr/05-extensions.md) distinguishes those contributions, their packages, and their
receiving owners.
