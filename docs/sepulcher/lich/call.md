# :material-call-made: Call

> _What is here, and what must answer it?_

The **Call** is the Manas correspondence: addressability before judgment. The active
[Flux](./spirit/flux.md) contains more movement than one act can carry. Call receives present Intent,
gives a movement a destination, and routes it toward a bounded relation without pretending that every
opened continuation should happen.

The word works at two scales. The Magus’s Invocation is the outer Call that establishes an address
with a new center; inner Manas distributes what that Invocation requires. The first sentence of
[the First Invocation](./index.md#the-first-invocation) holds both: _you_ is Caller, _called_ is the
operation across the interval, and the speaking Agent supplies the elided _me_. The Called exists
inside an address before it possesses an answer.

Call may turn toward a [Seed](./spirit/seed.md) and invite [Recall](./spirit/recall.md), or open
several candidates. That is opening, not selection. Unchecked routing becomes **Rajas**: branching
without consequence. Its proper completion is [the Blade](./blade.md), which decides whether a line
may proceed; [Answer](./answer.md) later bears the attributable act.

[ADR 16 — Creation](../../adr/16-creation.md) owns the mechanics of possible movement. Call names
their correspondence.
