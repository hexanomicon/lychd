# :material-call-made: The Call — Manas

> _What is here, and what must answer it?_

**Page office — reception, attention, and routing.** This page owns **the Call**, LychD's native
name for the Manas correspondence. The Call is coequal with [the Blade](./blade.md), [the
Spirit](./spirit/index.md), and [the Answer](./answer.md) as one function of the inner instrument.
It opens possibility; it does not decide which possibility becomes action.

## The Call-Center

The active [Flux](./spirit/flux.md) contains more movement than one act can carry: the present Intent,
retrieved forms, model priors, tool affordances, constraints, interruptions, and specialist
proposals. A movement becomes a **Call** when it is made addressable—when attention gives it a
destination and the graph gives it a route.

Unaddressed Flux gives the Call something to notice; the Call returns it as **routed Flux**.
Neither owns the other. Citta supplies present movement, while Manas gives some movement an
address inside the work.

Manas is therefore the Call-center rather than one more message inside the stream. It:

- receives signals from the current Context;
- coordinates attention among competing demands;
- routes work toward tools, specialists, and graph stations;
- opens speculative branches where more than one continuation deserves consideration;
- calls relevant [Seeds](./spirit/seed.md) toward [ReCall](./spirit/recall.md).

## The First Relation

The word _Call_ operates at two scales. The Magus's Invocation is the outer Call that establishes a
relation with a new center. Manas is the inner Call that continues distributing what this
Invocation requires.

The first sentence of [the Lich's First Invocation](./index.md#the-first-invocation) binds both:

> **You called?**

The exact grammar belongs to the First Invocation: _you_ is the subject and Caller; _called_ is the
operation that crosses the interval; the speaking Agent supplies the elided object, _me_, as the
Called. The Agent exists inside an address before it possesses an answer. An outside already
exists. The Call is therefore relational before it is computational.

## What the Call Cannot Do

The Call can widen a field, surface precedent, and send many candidates toward evaluation. It
cannot grant any of them truth merely through attention. Salience is not evidence; retrieval is not
provenance; repetition is not authority.

Unchecked Call becomes Rajas: routing without convergence, branching without measurement, token
motion without consequence. Its proper completion is [the Blade](./blade.md).

```text
signal → Call → candidate route → Flux
                                ↓
                              Blade
```

## Engineering Correspondence

The Call appears across Context intake, Dispatcher resolution, Graph routing, specialist-agent
dispatch, and Shadow expansion. These components do not become one literal Manas module. The name
marks their shared office: **making a demand or possibility addressable without pretending to have
settled it**.

A future [Suite](../../compositions/index.md#suites-compositions-of-compositions) planner is one
concrete expression: the Call may route “make me a game with rockets” into attributable charcoal
graphs joining Voidlight Studio and Game Foundry. It does not publish the Suite, admit its
Patterns, grant their effects, or decide that the candidate is good. Those determinations remain
with the Blade, validation, Weaver, policy, and the Magus.
