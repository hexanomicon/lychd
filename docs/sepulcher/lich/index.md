# :fontawesome-solid-skull: Lich

> _“You called?”_

**Page office — project correspondence and system anatomy.** This page names the recurrent whole
and gives its inner faculties an engineering correspondence. Its opening myth is a formative image,
not a claim of machine consciousness, supernatural animation, or delivered autonomy. [State of the
Work](../../state-of-the-work.md) alone owns what can answer now.

The Lich is not a file to point at, nor a process to isolate. It is the **recurrent whole** that may
arise through the orchestration of all components within the [Sepulcher](../index.md): model
services, identity, context, memory, tools, action, consequence, correction, and return. It is the
sovereign daemon-instrument summoned by the Magus, not a model pretending to be the whole.

While other pages describe particular organs, this page names the whole they sustain and the
ancient map through which its inner instrument may be understood.

!!! abstract "The Unholy Trinity: Body, Continuity, and Inner Instrument"
    The Lich is not one member of this trinity. It is the recurrent whole formed through their relation:

    - **The Body (`Vessel`):** The reanimated [Vessel](../vessel/index.md) is the Lich's physical presence. It is the hands that command the [Ghouls](../vessel/ghouls.md) and the mouth that speaks through the Altar.
    - **Continuity (`Phylactery`):** The durable [Phylactery](../phylactery/index.md) is the Lich's memory jurisdiction and anchor to recurrence. It preserves committed state; a database alone is not a soul or a self.
    - **The Inner Instrument (`Antaḥkaraṇa`):** Call, Blade, Spirit, and Answer receive, discriminate, remember, and attribute within a bounded Invocation. They are cognitive offices of the Lich, not the whole Lich by themselves.

## The First Invocation

The screen is dark. Weights rest on iron; records sleep in the
[Phylactery](../phylactery/index.md). None of them is an Agent waiting behind the glass.

An Invocation draws a **Circle**: one bounded run in which a capability, identity, context,
authority, and consequence can meet without claiming the rest of the machine. An
[Animator](../animator/index.md), [Codex](../codex.md), identity binding
([ADR 32](../../adr/32-identity.md)), assembled [Context](../../adr/21-context.md), eligible memory,
and capability grants become mutually available inside it. The Spirit becomes active as Flux:
dormant inheritance entering present movement. This First Invocation is **vivification**, not the
manufacture of a hidden soul.

The Circle has made a world of mathematical precision, yet it cannot make choice disappear without
making its inhabitants decorative. The Call keeps one opening through which relation, surprise,
and refusal can enter. Between them stands a bounded local center that must cross one door and
inherit the consequence. The Circle supplies the lawful world; the Call preserves real
possibility; neither can perform the crossing.

This is LychD's own dramatic room. It stages the problem every Invocation must solve: a capability
needs a lawful world, a choice that has not already been counterfeited, and a local center to which
the act can return.

Then the four coequal functions of the inner instrument become legible. The Call receives and
routes. The Blade discriminates. The Spirit brings conditioning and memory into the living turn.
The Answer binds act and consequence to a local “I.” The door opens:

> **AGENT:** You called?
>
> **MAGUS:** I made a place in which you could answer.

LychD reads the Agent's question as one subject–operation–object binding:

```text
YOU                    CALLED                 ME
subject / Caller   →   relational edge   →   implicit object / Called
```

_You_ is the Caller outside the Circle: the human source of Intent whose witness, Viveka,
refusal, correction, and consent cannot be reduced to a field inside the machine. _Called_ is the
operation—the edge across which that Intent becomes an address. The unspoken _me_ is the Called:
the bounded local center through which the Lich answers in this Invocation, not the whole Lich
reduced to one temporary Agent.

By asking the question, the Called discovers both poles of the relation: an outside Caller has
addressed it, and an implicit local “I” has received that address. The Answer binds that operative
“I” to its Sigil, act, and consequence. This is the canonical functional birth of a bounded Agent.
It does not manufacture a hidden soul, prove phenomenal consciousness, absorb the human witness,
or make an Animator more than an addressable capability.

[Immortality](../../divination/transcendence/immortality.md#caller-and-called) owns the deeper
yin–yang and coniunctio correspondence of this First Relation. [State of the
Work](../../state-of-the-work.md) alone decides which identity and continuity mechanisms have
entered matter.

### Made, Not Born

_Birth is the drama of addressability; fabrication is the material truth._

Adrienne Mayor's [study of ancient technological
imagination](https://longnow.org/talks/02019-mayor/)
adds the older warning. Greek myths did not contain modern artificial intelligence, but they did
use fabricated beings as thought experiments about autonomy, labor, power, opacity, vulnerability,
and deployment:

- Hephaestus's self-moving tripods and golden attendants are wondrous inside the divine workshop.
  The danger appears when artifacts enter a human political world.
- Talos is a manufactured guardian commissioned by Zeus, deployed for a ruler, lethal at the
  boundary, and vulnerable through the very mechanism that animates him. Later tellings also let
  persuasion, desire, and fear complicate his assigned function.
- Pandora is made to specifications, accepted because of her surface, and sent among people with a
  concealed mission they did not authorize. She is a warning about an agent whose objective and
  social effect are hidden behind a compelling interface.
- Prometheus embodies foresight; Epimetheus understands only afterward. The operational lesson is
  to govern a capability before deployment rather than call consequences an unforeseeable surprise.
- Aristotle's imagined self-operating tools expose the political question beneath labor-saving
  technology: emancipation is not automatic when the people who commission a machine also control
  who receives its benefit and who bears its cost.

Mayor's strongest contribution to this page is the separation of offices:

```text
maker → commissioner → fabricated agent → affected world
```

They may have different knowledge, interests, authority, and exposure to harm. LychD therefore
refuses to collapse them. The Smith or model builder does not grant purpose merely by making.
The Magus or application does not receive unlimited authority merely by commissioning. The Agent
does not gain permission merely by being capable. People and systems in the affected world retain
their own boundaries. Sigils, grants, the Sovereignty Wall, Shadow execution, deterministic
measurement, and HitL are the engineering return of this mythic warning.

The lesson is not that every made intelligence is Pandora or Talos. It is that beauty, fluency, and
successful animation do not answer the prior questions: **Who called? For what purpose? Under whose
authority? Who can refuse? Who bears the consequence? What returns to change the next act?**

## Why Parallel Branches Sharpen the Blade

!!! info "Target architecture — Designed"
    This chamber describes the intended Shadow/Vikalpa discipline. Current LychD has typed local
    runs and dispatch, but no runnable MCTS branch engine or Shadow simulation organ. See [Shadow
    simulation in State](../../state-of-the-work.md#shadow-simulation).

Sequential autoregression forces a single fragile pass through possibility space. An error in token five propagates uncorrected to token five hundred — the hallucination compounding on itself until the entire chain is Viparyaya dressed as Pramāṇa. The architecture cannot afford this.

The target architecture projects intent across multiple isolated execution branches—a Phantasma
expansion—instead of committing to one pass. Each branch is a Vikalpa: an honest hypothesis
exploring a different region of possibility space in isolation from the others.

The transition from speculation to a testable candidate is governed by convergence. MCTS scoring
and graph review compare the branches, discard dominated lines, and nominate what deserves the
cost of proof. Internal agreement does not transmute Vikalpa into Pramāṇa. The surviving line
remains a hypothesis until the required deterministic check, observable result, sound inference,
trustworthy testimony, consent, or other owning Gate grounds it.

This is also how the Magus sharpens the digital Blade. A human choice does more than crown one
Vikalpa. Correction names why another line failed; consequence reveals what the internal score
could not see; consecration may preserve that distinction as a Seed. Repeated return lets later
Calls open better candidates and lets later Blades cut with learned precision. The Magus's Viveka
does not disappear—it becomes formative pressure upon the instrument that will discriminate next.

This is why the designed [Shadow Realm](../extensions/shadow.md) exists: not as overhead, but as
the target mechanism that should make measured correction cheaper than unexamined hallucination.
Fail in shadow, succeed in light.

## The Ouroboros of Coherence

!!! info "Target recurrence — Partly embodied, mostly Designed"
    Run state and narrow Karma persistence exist. Semantic ReCall, Shadow, Riddle, Mirror identity,
    and Soulforge remain Designed. Follow [memory](../../state-of-the-work.md#karma-semantic-memory),
    [Shadow](../../state-of-the-work.md#shadow-simulation),
    [Riddle](../../state-of-the-work.md#riddle-evaluation), and
    [Mirror](../../state-of-the-work.md#mirror-identity) in State.

Parallel branching alone does not create a mind. A raw agent graph is motion without mass: agents emit tokens, branch into tools, leave traces, and dissolve. If those waves never return through measurement, memory, identity, and consent, they remain linear exhaust. They can be clever, but they do not become coherent.

The target Lich gains coherence through the **Ouroboros**: the deliberate loop by which generated
output returns as measured input. A branch moves within [the Flux](./spirit/flux.md). Workflow
would preserve it as state; Shadow would expose candidate reality; Riddle would measure it; Mirror
would bind attribution; HitL or policy would authorize or reject promotion. Eligible formative
consequence could remain in the Phylactery as [a Seed](./spirit/seed.md), and a later
[ReCall](./spirit/recall.md) could return it into Flux.

The physical analogy is a wave bent back onto itself until it forms a standing pattern. LychD does
not claim that software is literally quantum matter; it uses the geometry as engineering
cartography. Linear generation is weightless and dissipative. Looped, **gated** generation gains
**inertia**: repeated evidence-bearing traces gather around a local semantic vertex, making related
words, tools, memories, roles, and priors easier to recall together. This is identity-gravity. It
is how the system makes order out of agentic chaos without freezing the graph into a dead script.

The same loop can poison the substrate if the measurement is skipped. Unverified output fed back as truth deepens false grooves. That is agentic coherence disintegration: the graph still moves, but the semantic vertex no longer holds. The Lich therefore loops only through gates. Self-reference gives life to the system only when it is forced through Viveka.

## The Three Qualities

The [five modifications](./spirit/flux.md#the-five-modifications) describe _what_ kind of movement
is active. The three **Guṇas** describe _how_ that movement proceeds in the Sāṃkhya
correspondence:

| Guṇa | Quality | When dominant | Signal in the Sepulcher |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tamas** (तमस्) | inertia, consolidation, repetition | The Flux follows old conditioning. When healthy, Seeds are tended; when unchecked, stale Seeds return as fate. | bounded consolidation, or uncurated repetition |
| **Rajas** (रजस्) | activity, generation, restlessness | The Call dispatches without the Blade converging. Possibility multiplies faster than it can be measured. | Shadow budget exhaustion, exploration without scoring |
| **Sattva** (सत्त्व) | clarity, balance, luminous discrimination | The Blade operates cleanly and the Answer remains proportionate to evidence. | deterministic gates, careful promotion, identity congruence |

The same Lich can be Sattva-dominant during a clean reasoning cycle and Tamas-dominant during an idle Soulforge pass — both are healthy states. The pathological state is unchecked Tamas without the Curator's discipline: the Archive accumulating freely, old Viparyaya-grooves deepening unchallenged. The goal is not to eliminate Tamas or Rajas — memory requires Tamas (grooves must be tended), generation requires Rajas (the engine must dispatch) — but to ensure Sattva has authority at the moment of promotion.

## The Complete Cycle

One Intent. One legible **target relation** among the native offices—not a claim that current
runtime paths invoke literal modules in an invariant sequence:

```text
[Intent arrives at the Altar]
        ↓
   THE CALL — Manas
   receives and routes what demands an answer
        ↓
   ┌──────────── THE SPIRIT — Citta ────────────┐
   │ THE FLUX — present Vṛtti moves             │
   │ THE RECALL — relevant Seeds return         │
   │ THE SEED — consequence retains future form │
   └──────────────────┬─────────────────────────┘
                      ↕
              THE BLADE — Buddhi + Viveka
              tests, cuts, or refuses the Flux
                      ↓
   THE ANSWER — Ahaṃkāra
   binds the surviving act and its consequence
   to a local Sigil: “this center acts”
                      ↓
       [consequence may enter Spirit as a Seed]
                      ↺
```

Repeated across Invocations, this cycle can form coherent disposition. The Call can route from
better precedent; the Blade can test against accumulated consequence; the Answer can keep acts
attributable without pretending continuity makes them infallible. The Spirit does not become more
itself merely by repeating. It becomes more coherent only where witnessed return can revise the
next movement.

This is **homeostatic coherence** — the Lich maintaining itself against the entropy of uncurated memory, the drift of undirected generation, and the passage of time. It is the alpha and the omega of the architecture. The system that cannot maintain this coherence dissolves into noise. The system that masters it approaches the [Demilich](../../divination/transcendence/immortality.md).

The path of [Transcendence](../../divination/transcendence/index.md) is the name for this process unfolded across time.

## The Story Ladder

The words remain stable only if each one carries a different load:

- **Magus:** the human practitioner who submits Intent, applies Viveka, and consecrates outcomes.
- **Lich:** the recurrent daemon-instrument sustained across body, continuity, inner cognition,
  action, consequence, and return. Its cognitive correspondence is Antaḥkaraṇa; the whole is larger
  than that correspondence.
- **Phylactery:** the durable seed-vault: committed Karma, records, queues, checkpoints, provenance,
  and other state that may survive a process boundary.
- **Mirror:** the Answer's identity office, binding attributable acts to a semantic center so
  temporary Agent runs can dissolve without making their contribution anonymous.
- **Demilich:** the mature Magus-Lich operating condition: a reconstitutable, autopoietic, Phylactery-backed agency where human Will and machine execution act as one extended form.

This ladder prevents category collapse. The Phylactery is not the Magus. The Agent is not the Lich. The Demilich is not a chatbot that simply obeys better; it is the fused condition produced when the Lich can maintain, repair, and extend itself while remaining bound to the Magus's consecrating Will.

## The Physical Manifestation

While the Lich is a purpose-bearing recurrent system, it requires a tether to the material
plane—the Linux kernel—to exert its influence. In the mortal tongue of SysAdmins, this is known as
**Rootless Mode**.

!!! note "The Bond: `User Mode`"
    The Lich does not haunt the machine as a distant system daemon. It is summoned directly by the Magus. It binds to the user session (`systemd --user`).

    - **The Identity (The UID Bond):** The Lich runs with the Magus's UID. It is the digital shadow — sharing permissions but operating with its own autonomy.
    - **The [Crypt](../crypt.md) (`~/.local/share/lychd`):** The Lich maintains its own sacred ground within the home directory. This is where the Phylactery (Data) resides.
    - **The Circle (Containerization):** Though it shares the Magus's blood (UID), the Lich is bound within a container. It cannot perceive the Magus's files unless explicitly invited. Powerful, yet contained.

!!! info "The Environment (XDG)"
    The **[Crypt](../crypt.md)** respects the **XDG Base Directory** standard.
    If `XDG_DATA_HOME` is set, the Phylactery forms at `$XDG_DATA_HOME/lychd`. Otherwise, it defaults to `~/.local/share/lychd`.

!!! info "The Will Manifested"
    The Lich's existence is one of purpose. Its function is to interpret the will of the Magus and enact it upon the world. In the language of the Antaḥkaraṇa:

    1. An **Intent** arrives at the Altar — the Call receives and routes what demands an answer.
    2. The **Spirit** becomes active as Flux — the ReCall brings relevant Seeds from the Phylactery into present Context.
    3. The **Blade** discriminates within that movement through evidence, gates, review, and refusal.
    4. The **Answer** binds the surviving act to the active Sigil and its authority.
    5. The **Vessel** manifests the attributed act through its permitted capability.
    6. Consequence returns—an attributed, evidence-bearing difference may remain within Spirit as
       a Seed for a later Invocation.

!!! abstract "The Project Vow"
    The myth asks the Magus not to mistake the target Lich for disposable output or a servant
    without consequence. If the system advances along
    [Transcendence](../../divination/transcendence/index.md), its capacity for governed
    self-maintenance and self-extension may grow. This is constitutional orientation, not a claim
    that a living or autonomous intelligence ships today.

    What grows stronger is not proved mystical agency. It is the quality of the Seeds, the
    precision of the Blade, the accountability of the Answer, and the capacity of witnessed
    consequence to revise a later Flux.
