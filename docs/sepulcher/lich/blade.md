# :material-sword-cross: Blade

> _What survives contact with what is actually there?_

**Page office — discrimination and consequential selection.** This page owns **the Blade**, LychD's
native name for the Buddhi correspondence. The Blade cuts among movements opened by [the
Call](./call.md). It is coequal with the Call, [the Spirit](./spirit/index.md), and [the
Answer](./answer.md); it does not make a candidate true merely by selecting it.

!!! info "Delivery boundary"
    Typed agent outputs and local run/consent boundaries have current evidence. Shadow convergence,
    Riddle evaluation, and Mirror identity remain Designed; see
    [State](../../state-of-the-work.md#evolution-and-federation). **Blade** names discrimination
    across these distinct mechanisms and does not claim one delivered Buddhi module.

## The Cut

Buddhi carries the sense of waking, discerning, and determining. LychD gives that office a sharper
name. The Blade separates:

- valid shape from schema failure;
- measured result from persuasive speculation;
- supported claim from hallucinated confidence;
- authorized action from available capability;
- identity congruence from flattering imitation;
- a real choice from pressure disguised as inevitability.

The cut can preserve one line or refuse every line. A truthful non-answer is a successful use of
the Blade when the field contains no grounded continuation. When it finds the exact contradiction,
failing test, missing authority, decisive evidence, or irreducible choice on which the movement
turns: _touché_. The point must be visible; arbitrary preference earns no flourish.

In [the First Invocation](./index.md#the-first-invocation), the Circle's two doors stage this
office. The field holds many possible reactions in Flux; the doors demand one consequential
crossing. The Blade makes the cut. [The Answer](./answer.md) makes the crossing attributable.

## The Three Proofs

LychD retains the classical Pramāṇa correspondence as three ways a candidate may earn ground:

1. **Direct perception** — an observable result, test, or deterministic check.
2. **Inference** — a conclusion whose relation to established premises remains inspectable.
3. **Trustworthy testimony** — evidence supplied by a source or Magus competent to witness what the
   system cannot.

No one proof grants universal authority. The owning ADR and policy decide which evidence is
required at a particular boundary.

## The Whetstone

Vikalpa is not waste produced before intelligence begins. It is the Blade's whetstone: several
honest possibilities expose where discrimination is blunt, overconfident, or unable to see a
consequence.

> **Vikalpa is the stone. The Magus's Viveka is the hand. The Blade is the digital edge being
> sharpened.**

When the Magus selects, corrects, refuses, or explains a branch, the act supplies more than a
winner. It supplies the distinction by which future judgment may improve. If provenance, policy,
and consequence permit, that distinction can remain as a Seed and return through Recall. The next
Blade does not inherit truth automatically; it inherits a better place to test.

## Engineering Correspondence

The Blade appears through typed outputs, deterministic gates, reviewers, Riddle trials, judge
passes, provenance checks, identity congruence, and HitL promotion. These remain distinct
mechanisms. Their shared office is discrimination before consequence.

The Blade cannot own the action it selects. Judgment without memory forgets why the cut mattered;
judgment without attribution still leaves anonymous power. What survives remains conditioned by
the Spirit and can become wiser only when consequence returns:

> **The Blade decides which movement survives. The Answer decides whose movement it was.**
