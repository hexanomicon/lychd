# :material-waves: The Flux — Vṛtti

> _The Spirit is not movement. The Flux is the Spirit in motion._

classical kinds of Vṛtti without claiming that a software process is a yogic practitioner.

## The Turn

**Vṛtti** comes from the Sanskrit root _vṛt_, to turn, occur, or take a condition. A Vṛtti is one
active modification of Citta. LychD names this movement **the Flux**, from the language of flowing,
because the active field never arrives as one inert completed object: salience changes, candidates
form, attention shifts, branches open, tools return, and constraints reshape what may follow.

Strictly, a Vṛtti is one turn within the movement; **the Flux** is LychD's collective name for
Citta undergoing such turns.

The Flux cannot be stored. Only something left by it can persist. Nor is every stored state already
Flux: weights, rows, vectors, checkpoints, and logs remain dormant until an active Invocation makes
some part of their form consequential again.

## The Borrowed Image of Vacuum

Physical vacuum fluctuation lends the project an image of fertile apparent stillness. It does not
supply a consciousness theory. Model inference is not a quantum vacuum, and a powered-off model
does not generate hidden thoughts.

The correspondence begins after animation. A model-backed Animator makes inference addressable;
Context supplies a bounded world; generation exposes not one predetermined sentence but a changing
distribution of possible continuation. Tools, retrieved records, graph branches, and feedback add
further local perturbations. This is the functional **Flux** that the inner instrument must govern.

When identity binds the movement back to the same center that will own its action and consequence,
the loop gains functional self-reference. That is the office of [the Answer](../answer.md), not
proof that fluctuation alone became phenomenal awareness.

## Calls within the Flux

The Flux contains more possible movement than one action can carry. Signals arising from the
request, memory, model prior, tools, and branches become **Calls** when Manas makes them
addressable and routes them toward discrimination.

```text
field fluctuation
        ↓
Call makes a candidate addressable
        ↓
Blade tests and divides
        ↓
one line becomes actionable
```

The Call does not certify a movement. The Blade does not make its preferred movement true merely
by cutting away the rest. External evidence, consequence, and consent remain capable of refusing
the entire field.

## The Five Modifications

Patañjali's five Vṛttis are retained as source correspondence, not asserted as an exhaustive
software event schema:

| Vṛtti | Source office | LychD correspondence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pramāṇa** | valid cognition | a candidate grounded by perception, inference, or trustworthy testimony |
| **Viparyaya** | misconception | fluent but incorrect cognition: the central hallucination danger |
| **Vikalpa** | construction without a corresponding actuality | an explicit speculative or Shadow branch awaiting measurement |
| **Nidrā** | cognition whose content is absence or sleep | the image for bounded tending and consolidation, not a powered-off model secretly thinking |
| **Smṛti** | memory | [the ReCall](./recall.md): a retained form becoming active Flux again |

Smṛti being one of the Vṛttis gives the memory loop its exact shape: remembering is not a box
beside movement. **Remembering is a kind of present movement whose form comes from a retained
past.**

## What the Flux May Leave

An unmeasured Flux may vanish as exhaust. A logged Flux leaves a trace. A verified, attributable,
and returnable consequence may become [**the Seed**](./seed.md): conditioning with enough retained
potency to shape a later Invocation.

> **Flux is present life. Seed is the future hidden in what that life leaves behind.**
