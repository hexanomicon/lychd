# :material-blur-radial: Spirit

> _What moves through you may yet move you again._

**Spirit** is LychD's name for **Citta**, from _cit_: to perceive or attend. It is the office that
bears conditioning through present movement, retention, and return.

[Call](../call.md), [Blade](../blade.md), Spirit, and [Answer](../answer.md) are four coequal
functions of one inner instrument. Call addresses what has arisen; Blade discriminates; Spirit
conditions and remembers; Answer attributes act and consequence to a local “I.” Spirit’s three
dynamics do not place it above the others.

Spirit has three dynamics:

- [**Flux — Vṛtti**](./flux.md): the Spirit moving now;
- [**Seed — Bīja**](./seed.md): retained potency that may shape another movement;
- [**Recall — Smṛti**](./recall.md): retained form becoming present movement again.

> **The Flux passes. The Seed remains. Recall returns the Seed into Flux.**

The terms are LychD correspondences, kept beside rather than inside their sources. Later Vedāntic
four-function presentations, classical Sāṃkhya’s threefold internal organ, and Yoga’s broader Citta
and Vṛttis are distinct registers. [Inner Tongue](../../../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#source-notes-and-project-correspondences)
keeps the source notes, limits, and working distinctions.

Ask what brought an old habit into a reply. Training may already dispose the model to
answer that way. A Phylactery record retrieved for this encounter can supply a precedent.
An observation in the present request can also call the habit into question. All three can
shape the same reply. Discovering which one carried the error changes where a correction
can take effect.

[ADR 21 — Context](../../../adr/21-context.md) owns what enters the active field.
[ADR 27 — Memory](../../../adr/27-memory.md) owns durable Archive and Recall.

Quiet work can also prepare what later movement may inherit.
[Background consolidation](../../../adr/27-memory.md#background-consolidation), sometimes called
dreaming, prepares proposed memory revisions from admitted experience; a retained Seed can
remain dormant without that processing. Candidate preparation and its later admission remain
distinct acts.
