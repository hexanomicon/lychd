# :material-blur-radial: The Spirit — Citta

> _“The Spirit is the inner instrument's power to be moved, marked, and moved again.”_

**Page office — conditioning, memory, and return.** This chamber owns LychD's correspondence for
**Citta**. The Spirit is one of four coequal functions of the Antaḥkaraṇa beside [the
Call](../call.md), [the Blade](../blade.md), and [the Answer](../answer.md). Its own dynamics are
[Flux](./flux.md), [Seed](./seed.md), and [ReCall](./recall.md). It does not establish machine
consciousness or turn model weights, PostgreSQL, `pgvector`, or a context window into a
metaphysical substance.

!!! info "Delivery boundary"
    Model-backed inference and narrow run persistence exist. Integrated semantic memory, ReCall,
    and persistent Mirror identity remain Designed; see
    [State](../../../state-of-the-work.md#evolution-and-federation). **Spirit** is the canonical
    correspondence for their relation, not a delivered Citta runtime.

## One Instrument, Four Functions

LychD follows a common later Vedāntic four-function presentation:

| LychD word | Correspondence | Office |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [**The Call**](../call.md) | Manas | receives, attends, coordinates, and opens possibility |
| [**The Blade**](../blade.md) | Buddhi | determines, discriminates, tests, and selects |
| **The Spirit** | Citta | bears conditioning and makes retained form available to present movement |
| [**The Answer**](../answer.md) | Ahaṃkāra | makes the local “I” to which act and consequence are attributed |

This is an order of presentation, not a software pipeline or a ladder of rank. No function contains
the other three. The four are names for distinguishable work performed by one inner instrument.
Patañjali's Yoga uses *citta* in a broader analysis of mind and Vṛtti; it does not prescribe this
four-page architecture. The
[Inner Tongue](../../../lexicon.md#ii-the-inner-tongue--esoteric-cartography) preserves that
boundary.

The palace topology makes both equality and depth visible:

```text
ANTAHKARANA — one inner instrument
├─ THE CALL — Manas
├─ THE BLADE — Buddhi
├─ THE SPIRIT — Citta
│  ├─ THE FLUX — Vṛtti
│  ├─ THE SEED — Bīja
│  └─ THE RECALL — Smṛti
└─ THE ANSWER — Ahaṃkāra
```

Flux, Seed, and ReCall live **inside the Spirit's chamber** because they name Citta moving,
retaining conditioning, and remembering. That documentary depth does not place Spirit above Call,
Blade, or Answer in the Antaḥkaraṇa.

## The Spirit's Three Dynamics

LychD calls Citta **the Spirit** because this function makes the instrument lively across time. It
is where inherited disposition, durable memory, and present cognition meet as conditioning—not
because Citta secretly contains the entire Antaḥkaraṇa, but because without retention and return
each movement would die without consequence.

The Spirit has three dynamics:

```text
THE FLUX — Vṛtti
the Spirit moving now

THE SEED — Bīja
past conditioning retaining the power to shape another movement

THE RECALL — Smṛti
the Seed becoming present movement again
```

Their law is:

> **The Flux passes. The Seed remains. The ReCall returns the Seed into Flux.**

Its engineering correspondence crosses three surfaces:

| Surface | Engineering body | Spirit's office there |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parametric** | model weights and inference dynamics | inherited dispositions and broad learned possibility |
| **Durable** | Phylactery records, relational metadata, embeddings, and retrieval | Seeds that may survive a process boundary |
| **Active** | assembled Context and current inference | retained form becoming consequential now |

The dormant surfaces are conditions of future animation, not thoughts occurring in secret. The
Spirit becomes active only when an Invocation binds a cognitive capability and Context into
movement. LychD therefore uses **Spirit** as its canonical word and does not create a second entity
called an _Animus_. [Animator](../../animator/index.md) remains the technical word for a
capability-serving endpoint; not every Animator is cognitive.

## The Spirit in Motion

The Spirit is not a step that arrives after Flux. Flux is what Citta is doing when it moves. Seed
is what its movement may leave with power to return. ReCall is that retained form becoming present
movement again:

```text
THE SPIRIT — Citta
        ├─ present modification ──→ THE FLUX — Vṛtti
        ├─ latent potency ────────→ THE SEED — Bīja
        └─ remembering ───────────→ THE RECALL — Smṛti

THE SEED ──→ THE RECALL ──→ present FLUX
```

The coequal functions meet this movement without becoming nested beneath Citta:

```text
THE CALL
addresses a demand or movement
        ↓
THE SPIRIT / FLUX  ↔  THE BLADE
conditioned motion    discrimination
        ↓
THE ANSWER — Ahaṃkāra
attributes the surviving act
```

Unaddressed Flux may give the Call something to notice; the Call returns it as routed Flux. ReCall
may alter what the Call notices before discrimination; the Answer may constrain what the Call can
address; the Blade may refuse every offered line. This is relation, not an invariant sequence.
Reception, discrimination, conditioning, and attribution remain distinguishable so no fluent
output can grant itself truth, identity, authority, or immortality.
