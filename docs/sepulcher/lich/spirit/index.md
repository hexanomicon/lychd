# :material-blur-radial: Spirit

> _“The Spirit is the inner instrument's power to be moved, marked, and moved again.”_

**Spirit** is LychD's name for **Citta**, from _cit_: to perceive or attend. It is the office that
bears conditioning through present movement, retention, and return.

[Call](../call.md), [Blade](../blade.md), Spirit, and [Answer](../answer.md) are four coequal
functions of one inner instrument. Call addresses what has arisen; Blade discriminates; Spirit
conditions and remembers; Answer attributes act and consequence to a local “I.” Spirit’s three
dynamics do not place it above the others.

Spirit has three dynamics:

- [**Flux — Vṛtti**](./flux.md): the Spirit moving now;
- [**Seed — Bīja**](./seed.md): retained potency that may shape another movement;
- [**Recall — Smṛti**](./recall.md): retained form becoming present movement again.

> **The Flux passes. The Seed remains. Recall returns the Seed into Flux.**

The terms are LychD correspondences, kept beside rather than inside their sources. Later Vedāntic
four-function presentations, classical Sāṃkhya’s threefold internal organ, and Yoga’s broader Citta
and Vṛttis are distinct registers. [Inner Tongue](../../../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#correspondences)
keeps the lineage and working distinctions.

One office crosses three surfaces: parametric inheritance (model dispositions), durable forms
(Phylactery records and governed retrieval), and active assembled Context. These describe how
retained form may matter, not a single storage mechanism. [ADR 21 — Context](../../../adr/21-context.md)
owns the active field; [ADR 27 — Memory](../../../adr/27-memory.md) owns durable Archive and Recall.
