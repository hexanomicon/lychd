# :material-restore: Recall

> _The Call summons what is present. Recall summons what remains._

**Recall** is retained form becoming present movement again, not retrieval of a stored
[Flux](./flux.md). **Smṛti** comes from _smṛ_: to remember or call to mind. In Patañjali’s map it is
itself a Vṛtti: retained cognition becoming present cognition.

A search returns a note about a decision like today's, made by someone else under different
conditions. Retrieval found a candidate. Relevance lets the present center recognize a
relation. If reading the note changes the next question, its [Seed](./seed.md) has
germinated in present Flux. Even a conclusion the reader ultimately refuses can move them.

Whose memory was it? An earlier act of one's own might warrant _I remember_. A witness's
note calls for _she recorded this_. If the author is unknown, _this seems familiar_ may be
all [Answer](../answer.md) can say. If the note is no longer theirs to use, the reader must
leave it there.

> **Déjà Vu: Recall whose tombstone has lost its name.**

A record can also survive as evidence of a past selection after the source itself ceases to
be eligible. In LychD's [bounded public-note profile](../../../state-of-the-work.md#karma-semantic-memory),
a receipt can identify what was selected. Reading the source again requires current authority
and an eligible revision. [ADR 27 — Memory](../../../adr/27-memory.md) governs that crossing.
Only what can enter the present has a chance to move it.
