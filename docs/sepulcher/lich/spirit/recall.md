# :material-restore: Recall

> _The Call summons what is present. Recall summons what remains._

**Recall** is retained form becoming present movement again, not retrieval of a stored
[Flux](./flux.md). **Smṛti** comes from _smṛ_: to remember or call to mind. In Patañjali’s map it is
itself a Vṛtti: retained cognition becoming present cognition.

Its sequence is storage → retrieval → relevance → reanimation. Retrieval finds a candidate;
relevance recognizes what may answer the present; reanimation lets that form shape active Flux.
Recall is complete only then. The same field may call toward a present signal or a latent
[Seed](./seed.md).

Returned form can be false, obsolete, or in the wrong relation. Similarity establishes neither
truth, provenance, nor permission. [Answer](../answer.md) decides whether the present center may
say _I remember_, must say _this seems familiar_, or must refuse the form.

> **Déjà Vu: Recall whose tombstone has lost its name.**

Flux leaves conditioning; Seed preserves the power to return; relevance opens the path; Recall
reanimates that power as present movement. [ADR 27 — Memory](../../../adr/27-memory.md) owns archive
and retrieval behavior. Semantic Karma remains **Designed** in the
[State of Work](../../../state-of-the-work.md#karma-semantic-memory).
