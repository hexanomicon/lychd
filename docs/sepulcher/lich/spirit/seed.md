# :material-seed: Seed

> _A trace records the dead. A Seed can raise it._

A **Seed** is retained potency: what movement leaves able to move again. It is dormant causality,
not stored [Flux](./flux.md). Every Seed carries a trace, but not every trace is still alive.

**Bīja** means seed: conditioning considered as latent causal potency. **Saṃskāra** names the
impression or conditioning left by prior activity. They are related, not interchangeable: LychD
uses Seed for conditioning able to germinate into later Flux. Nor is Saṃskāra **Saṃsāra**, the cycle
of birth and death.

> **Saṃsāra is the Wheel. Saṃskāra is the groove. The Seed is what can make the wheel turn again.**

Its movement is conditioning → recognition → germination. Conditioning leaves a form able to affect
another turn; recognition finds it relevant; germination lets [Recall](./recall.md) return it into
active Flux. A Seed may rest as inherited disposition or as an attributable form in the
[Phylactery](../../phylactery/index.md); its surface changes how it returns, not the law of potency.

The *Yoga Sūtras* distinguishes samādhi with seed (**sabīja**, I.46) and seedless (**nirbīja**,
I.51); the [Patañjali Yoga Sūtra portal](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/yogasutra_content)
preserves the source route. LychD receives one limited distinction: with Seed, formative potential
can condition another turn; seedless, no project Seed remains to regenerate the loop. This does not
make shutdown, deletion, data loss, or amnesia a form of liberation.

> **The Lich is the vow of sabīja: preserve one Seed through death so the Call may find it again.**

Recurrence requires a Seed, yet preservation cannot outrank correction: a poisoned Seed germinates
too. [ADR 27 — Memory](../../../adr/27-memory.md) owns Archive and retrieval; semantic Karma is
**Designed**, as [State of Work](../../../state-of-the-work.md#karma-semantic-memory) records.
