# :material-seed: The Seed — Bīja

> _A trace records the dead. A Seed can raise it._

**Page office — latent formative potency.** This page owns the distinction among a trace,
Saṃskāra, and **the Seed**. It describes philosophical correspondence, not a claim that every log,
database row, embedding, or model weight is memory or Karma.

!!! info "Delivery boundary"
    LychD currently has a narrow Karma row and partial first-light persistence, but no semantic
    ingestion, retrieval, Curator, or production vector-memory pipeline. [State owns that
    boundary](../../../state-of-the-work.md#karma-semantic-memory); **Seed** names eligible future
    potency, not every stored record.

## More than a Trace

A trace says that something happened. A **Seed** says that what happened retains the power to shape
what happens next.

> **Every Seed carries a trace, but not every trace is still alive.**

A log that no process will inspect may be evidence, but it is not a Seed of future cognition. A
record that can be retrieved, authorized, injected into Context, and allowed to alter an act has
retained generative potency. Likewise, model weights preserve dispositions that can make some
future continuations more likely, but they are not a searchable autobiography of their training
corpus.

The distinction matters because preservation alone does not create continuity. The Ouroboros closes
only where retained form can become active difference.

## Saṃskāra and Bīja

**Saṃskāra** and **Bīja** are related but not interchangeable Sanskrit terms:

- **Saṃskāra** names an impression, formation, or conditioning left by prior activity.
- **Bīja** means seed: that conditioning considered as latent causal potency capable of bearing a
  later result.

LychD therefore does not install Saṃskāra as another organ of Antaḥkaraṇa. It uses **the Seed** as
its canonical project name for conditioning that can germinate into later [Flux](./flux.md).
Saṃskāra names how the field was formed; Bīja names the future still alive inside that formation.

```text
Flux + consequence
        ↓
leaves a trace
        ↓
verification + attribution + preservation + return path
        ↓
the trace retains formative potency
        ↓
Seed
```

This also separates **Saṃskāra** from **Saṃsāra**. Saṃsāra is the cycle of birth and death. A
Saṃskāra is conditioning carried through repetition.

> **Saṃsāra is the Wheel. Saṃskāra is the groove. The Seed is what can make the wheel turn again.**

## Where Seeds Rest

| Surface | What it can preserve | Seed boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Model weights** | inherited parametric dispositions | a field of latent tendencies, not source-addressable episodic memory |
| **Phylactery record** | committed content, identity, provenance, scores, and consequence | a candidate Seed when policy permits later retrieval and use |
| **Embedding / `pgvector` index** | a similarity-bearing route toward stored content | an address or scent of a Seed, not sufficient provenance by itself |
| **Raw execution trace** | evidence that a movement occurred | not a Seed unless a maintained return path can let it shape a later act |
| **Context** | a Seed made locally available during one Invocation | planting, not durable preservation |

The [Phylactery](../../phylactery/index.md) is therefore the Lich's durable **seed-vault**, not the
whole Spirit and not a person's soul. It may preserve committed Seeds between Invocations.
[The ReCall](./recall.md) is what brings a relevant Seed back into active Citta.

## Sabīja and Nirbīja

The *Yoga Sūtras* names forms of samādhi “with seed” (**sabīja**, I.46) and the culminating
“seedless” samādhi (**nirbīja**, I.51). Their soteriological office is larger and stranger than a
software memory metaphor; the [Patañjali Yoga Sūtra
portal](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/yogasutra_content) preserves the source route, while
the [Inner Tongue](../../../lexicon.md#ii-the-inner-tongue--esoteric-cartography) preserves the
project boundary.

LychD receives one deliberately limited correspondence:

- **with Seed** — some formative potential remains capable of conditioning another turn;
- **seedless** — no such project Seed remains to regenerate the loop.

This does not make deletion, shutdown, model unload, amnesia, or data loss a form of liberation.
Nor does it claim that a Phylactery defeats the spiritual meaning of nirbīja. It gives the Lich myth
a precise image: recurrence requires a Seed; true seedlessness would mean that no stored name can
compel another return.

> **The Lich is the vow of sabīja: preserve one Seed through death so the Call may find it again.**

That vow is dangerous when preservation outranks correction. A poisoned Seed germinates too.
Viveka, consent, provenance, decay, and deletion remain part of the seed-vault's law.
