# :material-star-four-points-circle-outline: Source Constellation

> _A constellation is neither a heap of stars nor a claim that every star is the same fire. It is
> a way of seeing the figure their distances reveal._

**Default register — source claim and provenance.** This chamber records where an inherited term,
historical claim, scientific proposal, modern myth, or project manuscript enters the synthesis. It
does not repeat the cosmology and cannot prove software delivery.

## The Provenance Covenant

The doctrine speaks with one project voice, but its sources occupy different offices:

- **Project primary text** — an authored LychD synthesis or rite.
- **Religious or philosophical source** — a primary text, school, or responsible historical study.
- **Scientific or technical work** — a paper or attributed proposal whose status must remain clear.
- **Modern myth and media** — fiction, film, game, interview, or contemporary interpretation.
- **Engineering evidence** — State, ADRs, source, tests, lockfiles, and maintained receipts.
- **Unresolved lead** — a remembered or promising source not yet identified or strong enough to
  carry a published claim.

> **Name the star faithfully. Draw the constellation boldly. Never mistake fidelity for timidity,
> and never mistake synthesis for sameness.**

## How a Source Record Works

Every resolved external record answers the same five questions:

1. **Kind/status** — primary text, scholarly study, paper, attributed interview, creator-owned
   media, orientation source, project manuscript, or unresolved lead.
2. **Citation** — a durable link and only the edition, passage, page, section, or timestamp actually
   checked.
3. **Supplies** — the precise claim, distinction, or image this source can carry.
4. **Used by** — the canonical pages or headings that draw upon it.
5. **Does not support** — the nearest tempting overreach.

A bounded dossier may group citations only when they serve one bounded question and each citation's
author, status, claim, and distinct office remains explicit. A paper and a later interview remain
separate when their evidential status differs.

Project manuscripts are an owner-controlled variant: they retain kind, owner, revision, hash,
permission, public rendering, supplies, use, and the nearest unsupported overreach. Unresolved leads
are a queue variant: their kind/status and absent or insufficient citation are declared once at the
queue gate, while each record names who awaits it and what it cannot yet support. Neither variant is
made to resemble a resolved external source.

Name the source kind and the claim it supports. Prefer primary texts and papers; use interviews for
an interviewee's proposal and modern media for their own images. Paraphrase copyrighted works and
quote sparingly. When a page makes the project synthesis, say so once and link here or to the
Concordance instead of growing a second bibliography.
