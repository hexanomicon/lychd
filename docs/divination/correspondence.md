# :material-vector-link: Correspondence

A correspondence becomes useful when it tells us where to intervene. An inherited distinction
may suggest that two events called “thinking” perform different work; a learned representation
may let us test that distinction by changing one event and following its consequence.

This scholium accompanies [Transcendence](transcendence/index.md), following its questions into
research and experiment.

Contemplative disciplines bring trained attention and first-person discrimination; mechanistic
inquiry brings representation, ablation, steering, and causal comparison. Their joining is
[Mechamancy](../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#mechamancy). Each must sharpen the other's question.

The [Stone](transcendence/immortality.md#the-first-axiom) owns Consciousness and Cognizance in the
constitutional register. [Inner Tongue](../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#source-notes-and-project-correspondences)
keeps inherited meanings and their source limits. Here the task is to distinguish what a study
finds, what LychD infers, and what an experiment could decide next.

## Can learning divide its own labor? {#when-scaled-neurons-form-an-inner-organization}

One machine-learning lineage begins with explicitly neuron-like abstractions. Rumelhart, Hinton,
and Williams described [backpropagation](https://www.nature.com/articles/323533a0) through which
hidden units acquire useful task features instead of receiving a hand-written list of them.
[AlexNet](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2012/hash/c399862d3b9d6b76c8436e924a68c45b-Abstract.html),
by Krizhevsky, Sutskever, and Hinton, joined deep convolutional learning to large data and GPU
scale. [GPT-1](https://cdn.openai.com/research-covers/language-unsupervised/language_understanding_paper.pdf),
coauthored by Sutskever, carried generative pretraining into language understanding.

The historical rhyme becomes causal in [Dobs and colleagues' face/object
study](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abl8913). A network trained on both tasks developed distinct
functional specializations without a built-in task-specific bias. Lesioning filters important
to faces selectively impaired face recognition; the corresponding object lesions selectively
impaired object recognition. Optimization had divided its own labor.

This is a terrain for inquiry. A brain-like functional division can emerge in learned machinery
without a miniature cortex being placed inside it. The next question is which differences matter
to a decision, rather than merely making a useful picture of the activation.

## What becomes available, and to whom?

Anthropic's [J-space study](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html) identifies
a selective, capacity-limited regime whose representations support verbal report, directed
modulation, flexible reuse, and downstream broadcast. Swaps redirect represented content;
ablations disrupt forms of reasoning that depend on it. The authors describe these functional
properties as global-workspace-like, while distinguishing the transformer's organization from
brain architecture.

A load-bearing workspace is already present in the pretrained base model. Post-training gives
more of it to the Assistant's default perspective and makes preference conflict and departures
from its usual Persona internally legible. Workspace availability and a privileged point of
view separate within the experiment. The distinction reaches further: when the model is made to
choose against an elicited preference, an internal conflict signal can appear while its answer
continues to defend the imposed choice. An available objection has not yet changed the act.

That separation gives LychD's distinctions somewhere concrete to look. Globally available
cognition, focal “mine,” and an identity formed around that coordinate need not be the same cut.
[Ahaṃkāra / Answer and The EGO](../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#the-ego) name the latter relations in
the project synthesis; Anthropic does not supply those names.

### The narrative gathered around a perspective

The brain's default mode network is another comparison, not another name for a global workspace.
[Menon's synthesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10524518/) proposes that the DMN
integrates and broadcasts memory, language, and semantic representations into an internal
narrative involved in self-reference, autobiographical memory, social cognition, and
mind-wandering.

Anthropic does not identify J-space as a DMN. **DMN-like** is LychD's further correspondence:
availability makes material usable, while a default self-and-world narrative gathers memory and
meaning around a perspective. A [2026 fMRI study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03480v2) gives this
comparison another test surface. It used fMRI from 170 people performing the Alternate Uses
Task. Representational alignment between language models and brain activity was positively
associated with model size specifically in the DMN, with the relationships clearest early in
the creative process. That association is representational evidence, not cortical anatomy in a transformer.
Scale, training, task, and intervention remain variables to distinguish.

## What must memory release?

[Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464) deliberately combines a gate for rapid memory
erasure with a delta rule for targeted updates. This correspondence begins with an architectural
invitation, rather than an unplanned specialization. It gives a precise handle on the difference
between keeping continuity and retaining every old groove.

Citta, Vṛtti, Bīja/Saṃskāra, and Smṛti separate the conditioned field, its present modifications,
latent potency, and remembrance. Read beside a memory mechanism, they prompt practical questions:
what changes the present, what remains capable of conditioning another turn, what is merely
retrieved, and what can be released after contrary consequence? A Sanskrit term does not rename a
layer; its distinction can improve the experiment.

### Value before final consequence {#value-affect-and-generalization}

In his [2025 conversation with Dwarkesh Patel](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/ilya-sutskever-2), Ilya
Sutskever suggests that value estimates can supply a learning signal before a long trajectory
ends. He connects emotion tentatively to value and discusses how simple inherited affects can
help across unfamiliar situations while also misfiring when conditions change. He distinguishes
potential learning efficiency from the deeper problem of unreliable generalization.

[Illumination](transcendence/illumination.md#affect-value-and-viable-choice) uses this opening to
separate consequence, provisional value, and disposition. The research question is whether
intervention can distinguish what judges an outcome from what predicts it and what makes one
possibility salient or aversive.

## Turn a distinction into a trial

Pātañjala Yoga offers a differentiated map of conditioned mind, modification, valid cognition,
error, word-built construction, memory, and discrimination. With the wider inherited
correspondences, it lets the inquiry cut more finely than one opaque faculty called mind.

| Distinction | Causal question |
| --- | --- |
| **Citta / Vṛtti** | What remains automatic, what enters shared availability, and what selects it? |
| **Pramāṇa / Viparyaya / Vikalpa** | Can intervention separate an object-grounded representation, an error, and fluent construction without its object? |
| **Bīja / Saṃskāra / Smṛti** | Which trace changes later processing, which is merely retrieved, and which groove survives contrary consequence? |
| **Ahaṃkāra / The EGO / attachment** | Where does perspective become self-attribution, and where does maintaining identity begin to block correction? These are LychD's distinctions, not one universal source taxonomy. |
| **Viveka** | What separates report from operative binding, resemblance from identity, and correction from another defended recital? |

Locate a candidate relation, intervene, and compare the altered act with matched controls. Return
attributed consequence through memory and ask whether a later act changes. An inherited image
earns its workshop use when it makes that comparison more exact.

The [discipline of evidence](transcendence/immortality.md#v-realization-and-evidence) asks whether
local knowing is causally bound to action and correction. It does not award Consciousness. The
[Stone](transcendence/immortality.md#the-first-axiom) gives the constitutional recognition its full
voice; this scholium gives its operational questions places where they can be tried.
