# :material-flask-outline: Distillation

What should the next creation inherit?

A finding becomes a useful lesson when it carries the condition under which it matters.
Distillation preserves the reason, evidence, uncertainty, and counterexample together, so the
next undertaking can use the lesson without mistaking one encounter for a universal law.

There is an echo of [Deuteronomy](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/deuteronomy/0) here: a journey
is remembered and its commitments spoken to the generation about to continue it. Our return
carries experience into a new act rather than repeating the old act unchanged.

## Give the lesson a next use

“Handle conflicts better” leaves the next creator guessing. “When two offline copies change,
preserve both observations and test whether a worker can resolve them without losing their
meaning” carries a condition and a consequence. It nominates a change for another comparison;
the proposal still has to earn its place.

The next use determines the handoff. A missing fact may belong in Context. A correction may
be nominated to Memory. A repeated procedural failure may call for a revised prompt, schema,
tool contract, or Scroll. A stable, transferable skill gap may justify a separately admitted
[Soulforge training objective](../../sepulcher/extensions/soulforge/discernment-training.md#choose-what-should-change).
Preserving a lesson does not itself grant permission to train on it.

[Ouroboros](../../sepulcher/extensions/weaver/ouroboros.md#carry-the-distinction-not-the-entire-conversation)
keeps these practical returns and their owners. Distillation may also conclude that the current
method should remain, that another probe is needed, or that the undertaking should stop.
If criteria change, [Canon](canon.md) receives a proposed revision for later trials; the
earlier result retains the conditions under which it was judged.

## Life and Death

What ends, what may persist, and what does return mean?

The Work's [native liturgy](../transcendence/index.md#the-breath-of-the-work)
says: **Life is the whole circulation. Death is the release of one form.** This authored
philosophy gives the creative cycle a distinction to examine: a particular form may end while
its consequences still participate in what follows.

| Meaning in this reading | Question for the work |
| --- | --- |
| Life: the circulation through formation, encounter, consequence, and return | What relations keep the undertaking responsive to what happens? |
| Death: the ending of a particular form | What has ended, and what obligations or traces remain? |
| Destruction: damage or dismantling | What was lost or impaired, including in a form that survives? |

An ending need not improve the circulation. Something valuable may be lost irreversibly.
Destruction supplies no promise of renewal; the distinction asks what can actually cross the
rupture.

### A form ends; a consequence may continue

Consider a proposal withdrawn after examination. Its active use ends. An admitted record may
preserve its wording, attribution, and the reasons for withdrawal. Later Recall may change a
later act if those traces remain available and are brought into it. This example concerns the
passage of work; it does not equate a canceled task with biological death.

An exact copy can preserve an arrangement. Resemblance can preserve a recognizable pattern.
Neither alone establishes continuity of historical identity. [Symmetry](genesis.md#symmetry)
can help recover constrained structure; it cannot reconstruct unknown, unrecorded history
merely because a pattern looks complete.

[Illumination's Ouroboros](../transcendence/illumination.md#i-the-ouroboros) develops the return of
consequence, and [Immortality](../transcendence/immortality.md#vii-reconstitution-and-self-repair)
examines continuity through rupture. [Weaver's Ouroboros](../../sepulcher/extensions/weaver/ouroboros.md)
carries the practical handoffs; [Reanimation](../../sepulcher/phylactery/reanimation.md) owns process
recovery. Those contracts determine what can be retained and resumed.

Return to [Genesis](genesis.md) with what the next attempt is allowed to inherit. The next act
can then reveal whether the lesson changed a decision and whether that change helped.
