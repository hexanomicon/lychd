# :material-flask-outline: Distillation

What should the next creation inherit?

One worker's observation vanished from the merged field notes. The next creator needs to know
which design was being tried, how the loss was found, and under what conditions it might recur.
That much can travel even while the cause is disputed. Preserve the evidence, uncertainty, and
any counterexample alongside the proposed lesson.

In [Deuteronomy](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/deuteronomy/0), the journey is remembered and its
commitments spoken to the generation about to continue it. Distillation asks what the next
undertaking should receive from this one, knowing it will face a world of its own.

## Give the lesson a next use

“Handle conflicts better” leaves the next creator guessing. “When two offline copies change,
preserve both observations and test whether a worker can resolve them without losing their
meaning” carries a condition and a consequence. It nominates a change for another comparison;
the proposal still has to earn its place.

Decide what the lesson should change before choosing its destination. A missing fact may belong
in Context; a correction may be nominated to Memory. A repeated procedural failure may call for
a revised prompt, schema, tool contract, or Scroll. A stable and transferable skill gap may
justify a separately admitted [Soulforge training objective](../../sepulcher/aspects/soulforge/discernment-training.md#choose-what-should-change).
Keeping the lesson and authorizing training on it remain separate decisions.

[Ouroboros](../../sepulcher/aspects/weaver/ouroboros.md#carry-the-distinction-not-the-entire-conversation)
keeps these practical returns and their owners. Distillation may also conclude that the current
method should remain, that another probe is needed, or that the undertaking should stop.
If criteria change, [Canon](canon.md) receives a proposed revision for later trials; the
earlier result retains the conditions under which it was judged.

### Preserve the way back to correction { #future-correction }

Suppose an earlier design had passed every one-writer case. “Local storage won” loses the
condition that made the verdict defensible. “It preserved the observation while one writer
edited. We have yet to try two disconnected writers” gives the next worker a reason to
reopen the design and a comparison to make. An unrelated objection about style leaves that
one-writer result intact.

Attach the decisive condition to its source, the evidence that could defeat the conclusion,
and what remains unresolved. The [proposed temporal comparison](../../sepulcher/aspects/weaver/drift/workflow-improvement.md#correction-after-distillation)
asks whether a later worker can still act on that distinction after the account has been
shortened or brought into another task.

This connects the Tree's [handoff relation](genesis.md#repair-the-relation) to Ouroboros. A
distinction must survive both a change of form and a passage through time. Omitted material
remains unknown, not refuted; a defeated objection may leave active attention while its reasons
remain attributable under the applicable retention rules.

## Life and Death

What ends, what may persist, and what does return mean?

The Work's [native liturgy](../transcendence/index.md#the-breath-of-the-work)
says: **Life is the whole circulation. Death is the release of one form.** A particular form may
end while its consequences still participate in what follows.

| Meaning in this reading | Question for the work |
| --- | --- |
| Life: the circulation through formation, encounter, consequence, and return | What relations keep the undertaking responsive to what happens? |
| Death: the ending of a particular form | What has ended, and what obligations or traces remain? |
| Destruction: damage or dismantling | What was lost or impaired, including in a form that survives? |

An ending need not improve the circulation. Something valuable may be lost irreversibly.
Destruction supplies no promise of renewal; the distinction asks what can actually cross the
rupture.

### A form ends; a consequence may continue

When the workers withdraw the latest-version proposal, its active use ends. An admitted
record may still preserve the rule as proposed, who proposed it, and the two inputs and
merged result that explain its withdrawal. If that record remains eligible, Recall can
bring the failure to another designer's attention. The old rule can change what happens
next without regaining authority to govern the merge.

The comparison with death concerns what may survive the ending of a form. Losing a life
carries stakes a canceled task cannot convey.

An exact copy can preserve an arrangement. Resemblance can preserve a recognizable pattern.
Neither alone establishes continuity of historical identity. [Symmetry](genesis.md#symmetry)
can help recover constrained structure; it cannot reconstruct unknown, unrecorded history
merely because a pattern looks complete.

[Illumination's Ouroboros](../transcendence/illumination.md#i-the-ouroboros) develops the return of
consequence, and [Immortality](../transcendence/immortality.md#vii-reconstitution-and-self-repair)
examines continuity through rupture. [Weaver's Ouroboros](../../sepulcher/aspects/weaver/ouroboros.md)
carries the practical handoffs; [Reanimation](../../sepulcher/phylactery/reanimation.md) owns process
recovery. Those contracts determine what can be retained and resumed.

Return to [Genesis](genesis.md) with what the next attempt is allowed to inherit. The next act
can then reveal whether the lesson changed a decision and whether that change helped.
