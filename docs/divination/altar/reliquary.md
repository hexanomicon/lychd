# :material-archive-star-outline: Reliquary

The Reliquary is the visible shelf of artifacts.

It is not the whole **[Phylactery](../../sepulcher/phylactery/index.md)**. It is the Altar-facing place for outputs the Magus may want to inspect, keep, compare, export, or consecrate.

The Reliquary holds:

- generated files
- reports and summaries
- retained failure evidence
- approved deliverables
- model cards, evaluation bundles, or training artifacts
- links back to the Invocation that produced each relic

The Reliquary gives the Magus a stable way to return to what the machine has made.

It is the output shelf, not the live execution page. Active motion belongs in [Scrying](./scrying.md); orchestration pressure belongs in [Nexus](./nexus.md). The Reliquary preserves what should remain inspectable after the rite has moved on.
