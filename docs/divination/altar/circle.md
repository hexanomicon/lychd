# :material-circle-outline: Circle

> _A world opens where the Circle closes._

An admitted **Invocation** opens a **Circle** for one answerable act. Caller and Called meet
within it with an Intent, Sigil, Context, selected memory, capability, and authority. Holding
these together gives the act a boundary within which its consequences can be attributed.

That boundary sits inside larger ones: a Bridge séance sustains a continuing relation, and a host
gives one Lich a place to answer. The same figure thus appears at several scales.

```text
Summoning Circle — this Lich's enduring host boundary
└── Altar / Bridge — a continuing séance
    ├── Circle — one admitted Invocation
    │   └── casting — performance of one exact Scroll
    └── Circle — another Invocation, with its own Run
```

Admission begins the Circle. Its **Run** gives the act durable execution identity, whether or
not a browser draws a ring. Several people, Agents, Postures, or model powers can take part.
Each retains attribution, separate authority, and the ability to refuse; meeting within one
Circle creates neither a fused super-Sigil nor an identical interior.

## The casting inside the Circle

[Spellweaver](../../sepulcher/extensions/weaver/index.md) admits the exact **Scroll**, an immutable
Pattern revision. Its placed **Spells** name the semantic actions the casting may perform. A
placement can request an Agent, tool, Gate, effect, delegation, or typed capability; admission and
authority still determine whether that request may be met.

An admitted [Animator](../../sepulcher/animator/index.md#animus-the-power-answering) supplies
**Animus**, active capability power through its Connector and exact CapabilityGrant. Graph gives
casting typed movement; model and tool returns remain candidates. Within Spirit's present
[Flux](../../sepulcher/lich/spirit/flux.md), [Blade](../../sepulcher/lich/blade.md) discriminates
and [Answer](../../sepulcher/lich/answer.md) binds the act. Many participants may help it arrive;
one local Answer remains responsible for what it binds.

## What Bridge can show

The compact **Circle** beside a reply shows supplied Run status and activity, with **Look into
the Orb** and an explicit **Cancel Run** for an eligible nonterminal projection. Expand **Circle**
to read the full Run identity, supplied Pattern revision and delegated-job identity. **View declared
score** opens Loom only when its supplied path matches that exact Pattern revision. These are
navigation hints; Loom still verifies the Run's pinned manifest before offering a contextual return.

Older retained replies may lack Run status or a declared score. Circle identifies those omissions
and offers the Run evidence link without inventing the missing metadata. A mismatched Run projection
is reported and cannot supply status or actions for another Run. Consent remains beside its own
request and decision controls.

Circle is the conceptual pinnacle inside Bridge, not a separate Altar instrument. No composed
Circle workspace, active-Spell map, Context/authority inspector, participant-attribution view, or
cross-instrument focus mode is delivered.

Follow [Bridge](bridge.md#follow-a-turn-to-settlement) to offer an Intent, answer consent, or
recover an uncertain offering. Choose **Look into the Orb** beside the turn to inspect that
Run's evidence.

A future focus within Bridge may gather the authorized views of one Invocation in one place.
Loom would supply its exact score and casting state; the account would show Caller, Called, Sigil,
Context, budgets, and consent; Nexus would supply capability and Animator observations; Orb would
supply bounded traces. Each view must remain reconstructible from its owners. A selected node,
animation, open stream, or model utterance alone cannot establish progress or authority.

## What can outlast the act

The Run remains ledger truth. [Oculus](../../sepulcher/extensions/oculus.md) and the
[Orb](orb.md) expose traces of activity, with their gaps and limits intact. A trace cannot replace
Run, consent, effect, or artifact truth.

Memory policy may preserve attributable residue as a
[Seed](../../sepulcher/lich/spirit/seed.md), a trace with potency to shape a later Invocation.
[Ouroboros](../transcendence/illumination.md#i-the-ouroboros) names that formative return through
evaluation, attribution, consent, memory policy and Recall. Repeating a Graph cycle alone does
not accomplish it.

## The greater Summoning Circle

In [Summoning](../../summoning.md), the Magus draws the enduring boundary around this host:
Codex, Crypt, local identity, Animators, mounts, secrets, and reach. Code builds the Bridge; these
choices determine the conditions under which its Lich may answer. They must become inspectable
configuration and security boundaries.

Each Invocation opens a smaller living Circle within those conditions. At either scale,
authentication, policy, capability admission, consent, and effect authorization retain their own
work. The symbol makes their relation visible; it cannot perform it.
