# :material-cash-sync: 41. Toll

!!! abstract "Context"
    Remote labor and local compute can carry cost, while money movement is difficult to reverse.
    A response code, model, middleware hook, or Dispatcher choice must never become spending
    authority.

## Decision

**Toll** is an optional economics Domain with two separated planes: a **Counting House** for quote
handling and accounting, and an **Irreversible Gate** for one independently verified settlement
effect. It is neither a mandatory wallet, global auto-payment interceptor, nor Agent authority.

HTTP `402`, an invoice, or any provider quote is an untrusted proposal. It may describe terms; it
cannot authorize signing, settlement, retry, identity, access, or a larger budget. x402, L402,
Lightning invoices, Wallet Connect, Nostr Zaps, and future rails are distinct adapters, not aliases.

Remote casting and teaching a missing Spell are distinct purchased resources. They cannot share a
quote or idempotency identity merely because one peer offers both. Payment satisfies neither peer
authentication, task admission, teaching quarantine, publication, activation, nor authority.

## Local credits first, rails later

The first practical topology is deliberately issuer-local. An operator may grant or sell
**MANA** service credits, or charge an exact request through one conventional payment provider.
MANA is an issuer-scoped entry in that issuer's durable Counting House ledger, not
cryptocurrency, cash, a bearer asset, a global currency, a governance vote, or a claim on another
issuer. An amount without its issuer is invalid. The initial profile is account-bound: unlike
issuers' balances are not fungible, and no cash redemption, peer transfer, or cross-issuer
conversion is implied. Changing any of those properties requires a separately admitted economic
and legal contract, not a configuration toggle.

Tithe meters model-token usage, generated media, concurrency, queue weight, hardware time, and
other resource consumption. The Counting House separately owns MANA balances, reservations,
debits, credits, refunds, and reconciliation. Only an issuer-authorized accounting boundary—not an
Agent, model, or ordinary tool—may issue, expire, or correct MANA. Every entry retains its authority
and grant reason or exact external funding identity; corrections append compensating entries rather
than rewriting history, while reservations prevent double-spend and negative available balance.

Only an independently verified settlement admitted under issuer policy may fund one exact local
balance or settle one exact pay-as-you-go request. Funding atomically and idempotently binds the
external-effect identity; a client claim, webhook alone, pending authorization, or ambiguous
settlement cannot create reusable credit. A sale retains purchase asset, amount, rate, terms,
expiry, and original funding identity. A required refund returns through its governed original
route where possible; it is not a promise that MANA can freely cash out.

Toll remains rail-neutral but evidence-specific. Every quote, authorization, and receipt names the
exact issuer, rail, asset, integer units, merchant, and network or external-effect identity where
applicable. A conventional processor adapter and an x402 adapter are sibling rails: x402 is not a
currency and does not require a project-native asset. Adapters translate exact evidence; they do
not erase provenance or make unlike balances fungible.

## The commitment before the signature

The Counting House may parse a versioned challenge, normalize integer monetary units, compare
candidate costs, reserve a budget, and reconcile receipts. It holds no spend key. An authorization
binds authenticated Principal and budget owner, merchant/service, method and canonical destination,
request digest and purchased resource, asset/network, maximum amount including fees, expiry,
redirect policy, and idempotency key.

The [execution-road policy](28-workflow.md#execution-road-planning) considers quotes only among
roads that satisfy capability, privacy, policy, and resource feasibility. Toll owns judgment of
the economic terms and reservation of their exposure; [Dispatcher](22-dispatcher.md) binds an
already eligible capability. Policy rejects the quote, applies one matching bounded low-risk
standing authority, or parks the Run for live Magus consent over the exact terms.
Novel or significant spend requires [HitL](25-hitl.md). Consent means *this exact maximum*, never
whatever a later challenge asks.

Accounting reserves worst-case exposure before signing. The isolated Irreversible Gate revalidates
the pinned authorization and live reservation, enforces per-effect and rolling caps, atomically
consumes the reservation, signs once, submits once, and records a stable external-effect identity.
Unknown version, scheme, network, destination, amount, fee bound, changed quote, or expired
authority fails closed. No model or ordinary tool receives a wallet or general signing surface.

## Two settlement rites

Local MANA consumes no wallet signature or external proof-bearing replay:

```text
eligible request → quote → authorize and reserve local balance
→ execute → debit on the declared delivery condition / release / refund → reconcile
```

An external rail separately crosses the Irreversible Gate:

```text
destination-pinned request → quote → validate and reserve → reject / standing authority / live consent
→ revalidate → sign and submit once → one proof-bearing replay → reconcile settlement and delivery
```

After independently authorized settlement, the original connector can make at most one
proof-bearing replay to the same pinned destination; that replay cannot begin another payment.
Payment and delivery are not atomic. A settled charge can yield no useful result, and a successful
response does not make content true. Refund, credit, dispute, expiry, paid-but-undelivered, and
ambiguous outcomes are durable, visible states. An unknown settlement is investigated and
reconciled—never answered with a second signature.

## Budgets, effects, and restore

**Tithe** records currency-neutral model-token usage, generated media, concurrency, queue weight,
and hardware time against a stable Principal or service grant even with every payment adapter
disabled. A paid quota grant cannot mint or widen a Sigil, expose memory or tools, bypass consent,
or displace protected local work. Currency-free Legion work still needs quotas, reservation,
attribution, and evidence.

Quote, reservation, authorization, submission, settlement, delivery, refund, expiry, and ambiguity
form a durable idempotent receipt chain. Keys, signatures, bearer proofs, preimages, invoices, and
wallet credentials never enter prompts, ordinary projected configuration, logs, traces, or
artifacts. [Oculus](29-observability.md) can observe redacted transitions and reconciliation lag;
a trace is not the financial ledger. Restore cannot rewind money: spending stays closed until
external rail state reconciles.

Extensions may add bounded quote parsers, rails, signers, and paid Portal connectors, but not
arbitrary global middleware, unrestricted keys, rival ledger truth, or a bypass of host
authorization. Every external adapter pins one exact provider or protocol profile and conformance
corpus. The first delivered adapter need not be x402.

## Deferred shared and sovereign settlement

x402, stablecoin settlement, cross-provider clearing, a native token, a validator network, and a
sovereign L1 remain future candidates, not accepted dependencies or delivery promises. A native
chain is not admitted merely by calling it another rail adapter. It requires later amendments to
this and every affected Covenant after independent operators demonstrate a need that issuer-local
credits and existing payment rails cannot satisfy, together with explicit consensus, custody,
issuance, validator, upgrade, economic-security, privacy, recovery, abuse, and legal gates.

No MANA balance, contribution, usage receipt, test token, or participation creates any right to a
future token allocation, conversion, airdrop, cash redemption, or governance claim. Economic
balance, token ownership, and validator status cannot grant canonical-source selection or
Evolution promotion authority; [Packaging](17-packaging.md#forge-neutral-source-trust) and
[Evolution](18-evolution.md#future-quorum-roster) retain those separately governed human-seat
decisions.

## Delivery and consequences

This Covenant is **Designed**, not payment capability. No Toll package, quote/budget ledger,
reservation, authorization, signer, settlement path, safe `402` response, reconciliation, or
budget enforcement ships. [State of Work](../state-of-the-work.md#x402-payments) owns that public
boundary; [Toll](../sepulcher/extensions/toll.md) owns operated doctrine.

Delivery proceeds through these steps:

1. Establish immutable financial identities and integer money types.
2. Verify quote, budget, reservation, and receipt accounting without money movement, then prove
   manually granted MANA through idempotent reservation, debit, release, and refund.
3. Build and verify the isolated, capped Irreversible Gate.
4. Admit one allowlisted conventional payment adapter in its provider's sandbox, with evidence
   for crash, replay, refund, chargeback, and ambiguous settlement.
5. Only then admit production pay-as-you-go, seller routes, x402, or other optional rails.

Toll makes economic consequence attributable, bounded, interruptible, and reconcilable, while
requiring durable accounting, key custody, explicit parked/ambiguous caller states, and later
marketplace, refund, dispute, legal, and abuse domains.
