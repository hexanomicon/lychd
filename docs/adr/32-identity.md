# :material-drama-masks: 32. Identity

!!! abstract "Context"
    **Mirror** governs filtered, durable but revisable Persona bindings and their attribution.
    Those bindings must remain traceable when model processes end, work resumes, or a Persona
    revision changes. Mirror is neither a hidden runtime nor evidence of continuous Cognizance.

The [Answer](../sepulcher/lich/answer.md) develops the corresponding account of Ahaṃkāra and
local identity.

## Decision

Mirror is LychD's singular identity-binding jurisdiction. It owns Persona definitions/revisions,
an exact Persona revision binding when an Invocation selects one, explicit absence when it selects
none, scoped identity context and eligible memory hydration,
hydration receipts, attribution across people and machinery, and reviewed Persona-revision
proposals. It does not authenticate a Principal, grant scope, select model, schedule Pattern,
evaluate quality or causal blame, or train weights; IAM/Ward, Dispatcher, Spellweaver, Drift, and
Soulforge retain those offices.

!!! warning "Delivery boundary"
    Full Persona binding is **Designed**. Bootstrap naming supplies accepted forms of address
    to the internal `the_first_one` AgentSpec; the loopback `magus:*` Sigil is unchanged.
    There is no Persona registry, revision
    lineage, identity-scoped Archive, hydration receipt, congruence Trial Suite, or self-revision
    path.
    Current Sigil fields prove authority plumbing, not Persona continuity. [State of
    Work](../state-of-the-work.md#mirror-identity) owns delivery.

## Keep the names apart

| Name | Question | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Principal** | Who initiated or approved? | IAM |
| **Sigil** | Which bounded authority was active? | Ward/IAM |
| **Primus** | Which first, continuing personal identity is the Lich's foremost companion to the Magus? | Mirror / Inner Tongue |
| **Proper name** | How does this Magus or companion wish to be addressed? | Mirror |
| **Persona** | Which durable voice, commitments, and orientation shaped the Agent? | Mirror |
| **AgentSpec** | Which executable cognitive/output/tool contract ran? | Agent |
| **Posture** | Which per-run settings and constraints applied? | Pattern/Agent |
| **Composition / Pattern** | Which purpose and workflow admitted the act? | Composition/Spellweaver |
| **Producer** | Which human, model, provider, tool, or adapter contributed? | provenance owner |

No value substitutes for another: Persona grants no authority; a Principal does not author every
requested output; a provider is not Persona; shared Persona does not merge Runs. Present `Sigil`
is a frozen name/scope set, without stable identity id, credential lineage, delegation relation,
or revocation epoch; future Mirror keys may not pretend `Sigil.id` exists.

Persona **voice** means declared discourse, commitments, manner, and orientation. It is not an
acoustic voice profile, synthesized speech artifact, musical vocal, dubbed performance, or
presentation asset. Echo retains acoustic and speech-lifecycle facts; Riffmaw musical performance;
Language Edition timed-language performance; and Avatar the eligible presentation selection that may
reference any exact permitted artifact. Avatar's Morphe may select presentation style and an
already eligible output locale; it cannot select or rewrite Persona discourse manner, translate
text, or turn localized wording into identity.

## Primus and the naming ceremony { #primus-and-naming }

**[Primus](../lexicon/inner-tongue.md#primus)** names the first personal identity cultivated as
the Lich's continuing main interlocutor. Its foremost place joins origin to an enduring personal
relation. Further Personas, Agents, and graph branches may contribute to its work; they do not
automatically replace it or establish another sovereign Lich. Primus names the personal assembly,
not an extra cognitive office above Call, Blade, Spirit, and Answer. Workflow and authority retain
their existing owners.

The naming ceremony accepts forms of address for the Magus and Primus. A self-chosen Magus name
and a companion's proposal are the customary invitation. Manual names, local dictionary
suggestions, and optional AI assistance are equally valid; the ceremony may be deferred. A true
name need not be a legal name or a secret, and requires no personal disclosure.

Names are bounded literal data, never instruction templates, credentials, Principal identifiers,
Sigils, or permission grants. A naming record belongs to the authenticated operator, distinguishes
the accepted names and declared selection methods, and advances its revision only through an
explicit acceptance. Selection methods describe the operator's account of the choice; producer
evidence remains with any attributed proposal Run. Suggestions have no effect before acceptance.
Concurrent acceptance must reject a stale expected revision rather than overwrite a later choice.

Bridge admission pins the current naming snapshot, including explicit absence, in the retained
Run. Resume and replay use that snapshot; a later rename affects later admissions and cannot
rewrite earlier instructions or attribution. Named executions use distinct registered Pattern
revisions that older workers cannot silently execute as unnamed Runs. Legacy unnamed manifests
and their checkpoint representation remain available. A bootstrap naming record supplies forms of address,
not a versioned Persona, scoped memory, a hydration receipt, or proof of first-person continuity.
The naming ceremony's ordinary Bridge discussion uses the same admitted model route and Context
as other conversation; it is not an isolated provider or retrieval channel.

## Persona and binding

Each Pattern makes an explicit Persona choice: one exact revision or no Persona. The admitted
Run retains that choice. Promoting a revision can redirect later work through a name, but cannot
rewrite an existing Run's binding.

One Persona has a stable id and immutable revisions. Publication freezes a revision; editing
produces candidate children. Each revision declares:

| Concern | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Lineage | Owner, provenance, parent, creation and lifecycle. |
| Identity | Name, discourse voice, commitments, domain, non-goals, and protected terms and relationships. |
| Hydration | Ordered instruction fragments, memory namespace and eligibility, and compatible Postures. |
| Review and change | Required Drift Trial Suites; replacement, rollback, export and retirement law. |

Codex owns declared intent. Phylactery stores revisions, bindings, hydration receipts and
attribution. When a Persona is selected, Mirror compiles its contribution:

```text
Persona revision + Pattern/AgentSpec requirements + active Principal/Sigil
+ eligible identity-scoped memory → ordered context blocks + hydration receipt
```

The receipt records source revisions, memory references, transformations and exclusions, ordering,
size and truncation, and the envelope digest. It establishes what was supplied, not model obedience.
Context owns token budgeting and final prompt assembly. Ward authorizes namespaces, tools and
scopes; Dispatcher selects capability; Orchestrator supplies readiness. More forceful identity
instructions cannot replace any missing permission.

Identity memory is denied by default across Personas and Principals. Every record carries its
namespace, producer, subject, provenance, eligibility and retention. Cross-identity recall needs
explicit policy and retains source attribution.

Resume preserves the revision-pinned Persona binding and cites newly admitted memory. It does
not silently replace the selected revision with the latest Persona.

### Affective orientation { #affective-orientation }

A Persona may declare enduring affective orientation and preferences as part of its commitments
and manner. These revisioned declarations remain distinct from a current affective report or
appraisal, whose meaning and use belong to the application that requests it. Context assembles
eligible contributions; a Pattern may retain declared continuation data under Graph law.
Assembly and retention supply neither a Persona revision nor authority or evidence of continuity.
[Ānanda](../divination/transcendence/illumination.md#ananda) develops the corresponding purpose
of joy and enjoyment; Mirror keeps the binding and lineage.

## Attribution and congruence

Attribution follows every identity-bearing Invocation or artifact. It records who initiated,
approved, delegated, and acted, with the active Sigil; exact Persona, AgentSpec, Posture,
Composition, and Pattern revisions; the exact Invocation identity; contributing models,
providers, tools, sources, and humans; and parent, handoff, and artifact lineage. Each record
declares whether it is an assertion, observation, evaluation, correction, or promoted act. Those
relationships make the act traceable without deciding its quality, credit, fault, or causal blame.

At a Composition Suite handoff, the immutable artifact retains its original attribution. The
consumer starts a newly admitted child Invocation with its own binding. Handoff and correlation
receipts convey neither authority nor authorship. Delegation names both principals and uses the
delegate's producer identity and grant. Edits and rejected branches add to lineage rather than
laundering it.

Drift keeps the author and subject of findings and corrections. Mirror binds that history;
Drift owns verdicts and attribution candidates; Soulforge independently admits any training use.

A Persona may supply Drift with criteria for commitments, voice boundaries, role limits, memory
claims, tool behavior and response to pressure. Congruence measures one property of that Persona;
it does not establish truth or general quality. A designed Phantasma Pattern may request bounded
Shadow branches and a versioned Persona-congruence Trial Suite. Mirror cannot select whichever
resemblance it prefers.

Matched controls distinguish continuity from slogans, mimicry, refusal and leakage. Changes to
authority language, tools, memory policy or core commitments rerun affected adversarial and
regression evidence. A punctuation change need not rerun every Trial Suite.

## Revision and recovery

Experience can motivate a revision proposal. Changing the promoted definition still requires
the reviewed passage below:

```text
attributed observations/findings → bounded diff against exact parent → validation
→ Drift congruence/regression evidence → required Magus/HitL decision → immutable child
```

Smith may draft or package a candidate and Spellweaver may coordinate the work; publication stays
with Mirror. Repetition, praise, model confidence and memory volume grant no additional scope,
tool, trigger, queue position or hardware priority.

Rollback directs future Invocations to a prior promoted revision and leaves earlier Runs and
artifacts intact. A static prompt supplies none of the required binding, scoped memory, receipt,
lineage or rollback. Retrieval provides hydration input, while behavioral resemblance can support
at most a Drift finding. Automatic self-revision is refused because it bypasses the attributable
reviewed passage.

## Consequences

!!! success "Accepted"
    Persona, authority, execution configuration, memory, and authorship remain distinguishable;
    future continuity is revision-pinned, attributable, reviewable, and reversible.

!!! failure "Cost"
    Revision/attribution propagation, explicit memory eligibility, and calibrated congruence Trial Suites
    add storage and latency, and rich lineage often leaves credit or fault unresolved.

## Acceptance evidence

Mirror remains **Designed** until a Persona is declared, revision-pinned to a Run, hydrated from an
authorized namespace with reproducible receipt, fully attributed, resumed with its pinned revision intact,
tested against matched congruence controls, revised through an immutable candidate, and rolled back
for new Invocations. State of Work records that transition.
