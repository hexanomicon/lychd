# :material-gamepad-variant-outline: 31. Simulation

!!! abstract "Context"
    Alternatives are not reality. **Shadow**, Vikalpa's possibility-lineage jurisdiction, opens
    bounded candidates against a pinned base, preserves why they ended, and offers evidence to the
    boundary that owns reality. [Dancer](../sepulcher/extensions/shadow/dancer.md) opens doors;
    [Hunter](../sepulcher/extensions/shadow/hunter.md) tests their ground; neither chooses the exit.

## Decision

Shadow owns simulation Intent, branch lineage, assumptions and unknowns, expansion/review
separation, budgets and stop conditions, branch evidence/artifact references, eligibility packages,
and cleanup eligibility/receipts. It does not own scheduling, unsafe execution, capability
selection, readiness, truth, Persona, consent, live mutation, Memory curation, or training;
Weaver, execution, Dispatcher, Orchestrator, Riddle, Mirror, HitL, Archive, and Soulforge keep
those offices.

!!! warning "Delivery boundary"
    Shadow is **Designed**. Current source supplies only `ShadowSimulationConfig` with `mode` and
    bounded `max_timelines`: no Pattern, branch store, search provider, workspace manager, Tomb,
    Reaper, Vision contract, evaluation integration, or promotion path. [State of
    Work](../state-of-the-work.md#shadow-simulation) owns delivery.

## Records and branch law

An immutable **Simulation Intent** names owner, purpose, exact base state, permitted effects,
budgets, stop conditions, and promotion class. It creates stable **Branches**, each recording
parent, generating Invocation, assumptions, required/observed state, unknowns, resource owner,
and status. **Observations** name exact command/tool/predicate, environment, inputs, outputs,
artifacts, and error class. A **Vision** packages one candidate revision, lineage, checks,
measurements, uncertainty, hazards, and proposed effect; a **Promotion Request** adds target,
validation, rollback, authority class, and expiry. A **Cleanup Receipt** records resources,
retained evidence, teardown, residue, and terminal state.

Repository, fixture, world, and artifact bases are exact revisions, never “latest.” Branches are
either text-only **idea** branches, isolated **material** branches, or a **promotion candidate**
packaged as a complete Vision. The latter is not selected, true, or authorized. Terminal truth is
explicit:

```text
open | blocked | failed | ineligible | candidate | retained | reaped | cleanup_incomplete
```

No branch need win. Exhaustion, contradictory requirements, unavailable state, and `UNKNOWN` are
truthful outcomes.

## Open, judge, prune

Shadow may use enumeration, debate, best-of-N, beam/tree search, human candidates, or another
provider; no heuristic is constitutional. Dancer receives the common Intent and one declared Lens,
cannot see siblings before the join, and returns alternatives with provenance, assumptions,
required state, and unknowns—never ranking or promotion. Hunter separately reviews one named
candidate and returns challenges, evidence requests, boundary findings, withhold recommendations,
and uncertainty; it cannot invent the preferred replacement. A join may cluster or deduplicate,
but preserves original lineage.

Before material spend, deterministic predicates may make a branch ineligible; witnessed policy
violations may block it. Missing evidence is `unknown` and may justify one bounded inquiry naming
question, access, budget, and stop condition; it ends `resolved`, `refuted`, or `unknown` and may
not widen itself. Model hesitation proves nothing. Riddle evaluates reviewer Posture against
matched solvable controls. The **Dual-Gate** combines deterministic checks with attributed,
calibrated qualitative judgment before eligibility; no score, confidence, or surviving cheap branch
selects reality.

## Material containment and budgets

A material branch receives an isolated workspace through a replaceable provider. Jujutsu can
provide tracked change identity, not an execution sandbox. Its contract pins base and owned paths,
mounts/processes/ports/services/network/secrets/effects, evidence locations, leases, reset/snapshot,
expiry, teardown, and drift verification. Effectful tests use an admitted execution boundary:
directory deletion cannot end processes, revoke credentials, close ports, undo remote calls, or
restore shared state. The designed Tomb proves none of this today.

Intent bounds branch/depth count, tokens, wall time, spend, disk, artifacts, concurrent execution,
and review rounds; children consume their parent's reserved envelope. Weaver coordinates fan-out,
join, cancellation, and termination; Dispatcher chooses capability; Orchestrator manages readiness
and lease conflict; Workers run jobs. Exhaustion cancels or marks incomplete, then cleans up—it
never selects a winner.

## Evaluation, reaping, and collapse

Each Observation establishes only its narrow predicate. Unit tests, commits, and model judges do
not respectively prove architectural fit, shared-state isolation, or an effect. Shadow submits
exact branches, environments, observations, and artifacts to versioned Riddle Suites; deterministic
receipts and verdicts stay distinct. Mirror may supply Persona-congruence criteria, never private
Persona selection. Any score is a versioned, attributable evaluation signal with declared scope;
it may prune, deprioritize, or converge inside the experiment but proves and authorizes nothing.

## The Branch Reaper

Only after retention settles may Reaper act: commit terminal state; retain required inputs,
observations, artifacts, findings, and errors; enumerate jobs, leases, processes, services, ports,
and credentials; have workspace/execution owners tear down; reconcile external/shared residue; and
write the Cleanup Receipt. TTL is not proof of safe deletion; hold, lease, pending promotion,
required evidence, or unknown ownership postpones it. Failure retains its witnessed boundary;
neither traces nor success/failure pairs become Karma or training examples automatically.
Jujutsu cleanup verifies descendants and retained references because abandonment can rewrite
revision relationships.

Shadow emits only a Promotion Request:

```text
Vision → target owner revalidates current state and evidence → rollback/snapshot preparation
→ Ward and HitL/preauthorization decide the exact effect → owner applies and verifies → receipt
```

The target rechecks base, preconditions, policy, authority, and rollback against current reality.
Historical success or preauthorization outside an eligible class cannot merge, publish, purchase,
deploy, restore, or otherwise cross the boundary. Archive separately admits memory; Soulforge
separately admits training. This is the Demarcation Law: Weaver coordinates live Invocations;
Shadow produces candidate realities.

## Consequences

!!! success "Accepted"
    Candidate worlds carry lineage, budgets, evidence, adversarial review, honest pruning, and
    cleanup without collapsing experimentation into authority or live effect.

!!! failure "Cost"
    Search and review consume finite resources, isolation/teardown are difficult, bias can converge
    on the wrong candidate, and some experiments end without a promotable answer.

## Acceptance evidence

Shadow remains **Designed** until one bounded Pattern proves independent Dancer branches, separate
Hunter review, base pinning, material ownership, deterministic observations, Riddle evaluation,
budget cancellation, inert Promotion Request, current-state revalidation, and a Cleanup Receipt
including incomplete teardown. State of Work records that transition.
